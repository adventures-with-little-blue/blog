# Evening Ride After the Orienteering Practice

Monday again, and once again orienteering at Måndagsbommen. Naturally with the motorcycle as transport. This week Malax IF had set the course in Sarvijoki, on the Jurva side (Kurikka) of the municipal border. When most others turned west back towards Malax after the orienteering, I turned east towards Jurva. It became a short and cozy evening ride in really pleasant weather. I had time to photograph a couple of churches, and I also had time to sit for a short while in the sun and recover.

<!--more-->

The road towards Jurva first runs through the village of Sarvijoki. There I photographed the prayer house. I turned off from the road to Jurva towards Jyrynkylä and Harjunkylä. These villages are, as it were, off the normal travel routes. I myself did not even know about these villages before I started moving around by motorcycle. Despite that, the paved roads range from acceptable to at times genuinely good. It feels a little unexpected that these small villages, in the middle of the forest, have such good roads. From Harjunkylä you continue to Metsäkylä, and then I turned off towards Närvijoki.

![Sarvijoki prayer house](sarvijoki_bonehus.jpg "Sarvijoki prayer house.")

In Närvijoki I turned west again in the direction of Pörtom. This road is in fairly miserable condition, even worse than the road between Metsäkylä and Närvijoki. Once in Pörtom I photographed the [church](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C3%B6rtoms_kyrka) (sv). Until 1973 Pörtom was its own municipality, but nowadays it is part of the town of Närpes.

![Pörtom Church](portom_kyrka.jpg "Pörtom Church.")

![The poor man statue](portom_fattiggubbe.jpg "The poor man statue at Pörtom Church.")

Then further south towards Övermark. Övermark too was an independent municipality until 1973, when it was merged into Närpes together with Pörtom. The road between Pörtom and Övermark and then onwards to Yttermark is, incidentally, a rather monotonous stretch. It is more or less arrow straight and almost completely flat. With only a few very gentle bends, it is 25 km of straight country road. It passes through Övermark by way of a couple of small bends and almost arrow-straight through Yttermark, but there are also plenty of houses scattered among the fields between the villages. The speed limit varies between 50 km/h in Övermark and Yttermark and 80 km/h between the villages.

![Övermark Church](overmark_kyrka.jpg "Övermark Church.")

Instead of following the whole of that rather boring straight, I turned off at Övermark church, photographed it too, and then continued in the direction of Korsnäs. In sharp contrast to the arrow-straight road I had just been following, this road currently offers something that few roads in Ostrobothnia have: it is narrow, winding, and _the tarmac is in excellent condition_! It is almost the only road in Ostrobothnia where you actually can and even _need_ to lean the motorcycle properly through the curves. The other winding roads have such poor asphalt that it is not possible to ride quickly anyway. Do watch out for loose gravel on the tarmac in the bends, though; there always seems to be some in several places along this road.

After the short winding section I took a break, sat for a while in the sun and rehydrated after the orienteering. In the village of Bodbacka there is a small barbecue area by the shore of Lake Hinjärv. Beautiful and very peaceful.

![Hinjärv](hinjarv1.jpg "Little Blue at Hinjärv.")

![Hinjärv](hinjarv2.jpg "Enjoying the sun.")

Then it was simply a matter of steering home. The clock was already past nine by the time I got home. This evening it was less than three quarters of an hour of orienteering and 162 km of riding.
