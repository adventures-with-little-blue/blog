# Panda Coffee?

Now it's really spring and no more ice on the roads. But despite it being almost a month since the premiere, the riding weather is hardly much warmer. There are frosts at night and daytime temperatures are still mostly kept around +10°C, and unfortunately the sunny and clear days have been quite few. This day the weather was quite decent with temperatures up to +14°C. Time for a ride, then. Today it was time to explore some new places that Little Blue hasn't visited and also some places where I myself have never been.

<!--more-->

On the equipment side, there were several novelties. Little Blue has gotten a new rear tire, a Metzeler Tourance. Then, installed as recently as yesterday, new rear brake rotor and new brake pads. And last but not least, my Christmas present to myself, a Bluetooth headset in the helmet, was finally installed and operational. Now I get navigation instructions in my ears, so this time there shouldn't be any navigation mistakes.

The journey started through Jurva, then Kurikka and then almost down to Jalasjärvi before the front wheel turned towards Peräseinäjoki. After a short tour in Peräseinäjoki city, I continued eastward towards Alavus. It became a short tour through Alavus center and then on to Tuuri to view Tuuri village shop - but only from the outside! Then a tour around Töysä center, via the church, school, shop, fire station and youth center. The village thus has everything an Ostrobothnian village should have.

![Tuuri village shop](tuuri.jpg)

Then a short tour through the center of Etseri, after which I continued to Etseri zoo. Unfortunately, this trip was time-framed by other events, I had promised to pick up my son with a friend from the movies in the afternoon, so I didn't take time to go and visit the pandas. Instead, it was just a cup of coffee at Hotel Mesikämmen. They didn't even have pastries to offer. Not much of a panda coffee if you ask me.

![Zoo](zoo.jpg)

After the break I continued towards Karstula. On the way I passed Väätäiskylä in Multia municipality and then Pylkönmäki in Saarijärvi municipality. Pylkönmäki, although a small village, was previously the center of its own municipality and offered not least a beautiful view over beautifully rolling countryside. Otherwise, the road was probably among the most boring I've ridden. Many kilometers of what was quite a straight road, forest follows forest and hardly any buildings.

![View from Pylkönmäki church village](pylkonmaki.jpg)

After Pylkönmäki the road is more entertaining. Karstula wasn't much of a big place either. Despite the town being beautifully situated between several lakes, you can hardly see any of this when driving through along the main road. Then I continued to Soini and then a detour via Lehtimäki. Here it was time to refuel. I was now running short on time so then it was off towards home. I allowed myself a small detour. Instead of going along the main route through Seinäjoki, I followed Isokoskentie to Nurmo center and from there I continued in the direction of Ylistaro. And then from Ylistaro along the Kyrö river to Kolkki and home. I made it just in time to pick up the kids from the movies.

![On the way home](mikkila.jpg)

This day it was 476 km. For the most part, the journey was fairly uneventful and the roads offered no surprises. But the weather was good, rear tire and brakes worked fine and otherwise a completely successful trip.

## New Municipalities

My long-term goal is to take my motorcycle Little Blue to all of Finland's municipalities. During this journey we visited these new municipalities:
* Alavus
* Ähtäri
* Multia
* Saarijärvi
* Karstula
* Soini

Now Little Blue has rolled through 63 of Finland's 308 municipalities.
