# Evening Ride with a Small Milestone

Tonight I gave my daughter a ride to a football match. While she was watching the game, I went for a short ride. The new thing for today was that I removed the ugly windshield. Now I have a real naked bike again, ready for the warm summer! We'll see how it feels, it's been a while.

<!--more-->

This time, Blue and I headed south. The idea was to get to know the village of Korsbäck, where we had probably never been before. And right there, just as we turned from the road Strandvägen towards Korsbäck, Blue hit a round number. Congratulations on *80,000 km* Little Blue!

![80,000 km](80k.jpg)

After that, we took a little extra ride with my daughter on the pillion seat.

![Sunset](sunset.jpg)

This evening's ride was 154 km. And how did it feel without the windshield? Pretty windy actually, and not really any quieter due to less turbulence either. I think I'll put the windshield back on, at least for longer trips. I think I'll manage better with less wind hitting my body, and the windshield also protects a bit from insects and partly from rain.
