# Synchronising the carburettors - again

In an attempt to cure the vibrations and a slightly reluctant throttle response, I decided - once again - to synchronise the carburettors. And this time there was actually a bit of a rush to get it done.

<!--more-->

I have now tried to synchronise the carburettors at least three times before - without managing to get it quite right. Part of the reason may be that my synchronising tool is not entirely accurate, but above all the problem each time has been that I have not managed to set up a reliable fuel supply. I bought a "workshop bottle" with a tap, but that blasted bottle (I previously called it China junk, but it is actually Made in India) leaks itself empty in just a few minutes. This time I took the time to try to fix the leak with two slightly too small hose clamps, and now it stopped leaking at the hose connection. But it still leaks from the tap itself - bloody India junk. This time it was at least dripping slowly enough that I judged it would work.

I removed all the fairings and warmed up the engine with a short ride. At the same time I calibrated my "before" mark. Then off with the tank, on with the vacuum gauges, and then I connected the workshop tank. This time I got the whole setup working reasonably well. There was a bit of petrol dripping, but not an unacceptable amount.

According to the gauges the cylinders were slightly out of sync, but not by much. Without solid experience I cannot say whether 0.1 - 0.2 bar at 2000 rpm is a lot or a little, or whether it could have caused any problems. In any case I adjusted the screw so that both gauges showed exactly the same, both at idle and at 4000 rpm. Then I settled for that and tore down the test setup again (I poured the leftover petrol from the workshop tank into the lawnmower). I was in a bit of a hurry to go and help with dinner, with guests on their way. Afterwards it occurred to me that I should of course have swapped each gauge over to the other cylinder to double-check the adjustment. Sigh. Still, the last time I tested them they actually showed almost exactly the same.

![Synchronisation](synk.jpg "Synchronising the carburettors.")

Once dinner was out of the way, I put everything back together and went for a short test ride and to fill the tank for the next day. During that short test ride it actually _felt_ better. It did not seem quite as reluctant on the throttle, and the uneven running was somewhat better (though not completely perfect). But I realise that a short test ride is not enough to judge whether it turned out well or not. I will have to pay closer attention tomorrow. Tomorrow, yes - that is when I head off on a trip.

![Packed and ready](packad.jpg "Packed for adventure!")

New tyres, air pressure checked. Wheel bearings ok. Brake pads ok (a bit on the thin side at the front, but they should do). Oil checked. Coolant checked. Battery freshly charged. Lights ok. The front forks freshly serviced, no leaks. And now the carburettors synchronised as well. Let us see how far I get: 50 km, halfway, or all the way back home again? It is usually the things I have not thought of that cause the real problems.
