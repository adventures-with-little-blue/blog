# Charly Sinewan: Riding through ALL the countries

A little something different this time. No content of my own, just a comment on someone else's project. Spanish YouTuber [Charly Sinewan](https://www.sinewan.com) has decided to visit ALL the countries on Earth by motorcycle. My [Finland/308](/finland308/) project is about visiting every municipality in Finland. My project pales in comparison. :smile:

<!--more-->

Charly has spent the last 16 years riding his motorcycle through Asia, Africa and, most recently, North and South America. To date, he has visited 79 countries by motorcycle. In any case, he reckons there are 195 countries in the world, so there are still quite a few left to visit.

Here he talks about his upcoming project himself (in Spanish). There is also a long YouTube version here: [https://youtu.be/5vBVrMqYyAI?si=hA3BO5uW5CzB3FV-](https://youtu.be/5vBVrMqYyAI?si=hA3BO5uW5CzB3FV-). Use YouTube's subtitles if his Spanish is too fast (at least I cannot keep up).

Finland is still entirely blank on his map, so that means he will sooner or later come this way too. I suppose I will have to get out on the roads to catch a glimpse of him then – big fan.
