# Tour de Pirkanmaa

This was most likely the last long ride of the year – a full day of sightseeing in Pirkanmaa. The days are getting shorter, so daylight hours run out before the desire to ride does. It was also probably one of the last real summer days of the year with temperatures over +20°C. The plan had been to spend the weekend renovating my son's room, but with weather like this, it's better to be outdoors. Renovation will have to wait until next weekend.

<!--more-->

I started already a bit before actual sunrise, though it was already fairly light. First came a transport stretch down through Jurva, Kauhajoki, and Karvia to Parkano. From Parkano I followed highway 3/E12 a bit south, until I turned off onto road 276 towards Luhalahti. That road goes east of Lake Kyrösjärvi, while the highway goes to the west. Luhalahti belongs to the city of Ikaalinen, whose center is on the other side of the lake. A pleasant winding road with small villages at regular intervals. At Inkula bridge, now in Ylöjärvi municipality, I stopped for breakfast and thermos coffee.

![Inkula bridge](inkula.jpg)

Just south of Inkula is Haveri, known for its closed gold mine. The Haveri mine has a water-filled open pit with old mine tunnels under the water. The mine is, or at least has been, a haunt for the cave diver community. Unfortunately, most of it is locked up, so I couldn't take a closer look. I didn't even check if the museum was open this early on a Saturday morning. I continued to Kyröskoski and Hämeenkyrö. Then I continued along winding small roads down towards Siuro in Nokia municipality.

Siuro is a cozy little village with narrow alleys and small houses built quite close together. The Finnish Heritage Agency has classified Siuro hill as a nationally important protected site. In (or maybe just next to) Siuro is Linnavuori, where Valmet's, later Sisus and now Agco Power's engine factory is located. There, engines for Valtra tractors and some other Agco tractor brands are manufactured.

From Siuro I continued into Nokia city and specifically to Tehdassaari (Factory Island). On this small island, the area's industry has grown. Among other things, the company Nokia's headquarters was here back when their main product was rubber boots. I had planned to have a coffee here, but the café didn't seem to be open yet. After a short walk around, I continued, now heading southwest to circle around Lake Pyhäjärvi. I passed through Tottijärvi, formerly an independent municipality, now part of Nokia city.

![Tehdassaari](tehdassaari1.jpg)
![Nokia headquarters](nokia.jpg)
Nokia headquarters in the early 1900s.

I made a short detour to Laukko Manor, a place with history dating back at least to the early 1400s. The manor is now privately owned, but in summer there is a summer café, restaurant, and concerts, exhibitions, and tours are organized. Unfortunately, the season was over and everything was closed, so I didn't get coffee here either. I continued and cut through Narva village in Vesilahti municipality. Then on to Vesilahti center, where I filled up Blue's tank.

The journey continued through Lempäälä and then out towards Tampere-Pirkkala airport. I had hoped to catch a glimpse of some military aircraft stationed at Satakunta Air Command, but no such luck. Instead, I continued into Pirkkala center and then on to Tampere. I took a walk in the park at Hatanpää Manor. This manor also has an eventful past, but nowadays it is owned by the city of Tampere, so people can enjoy the beautiful rose garden and the otherwise lovely surroundings.

![Hatanpää Manor](hataanpaa1.jpg)
![Roses](hataanpaa2.jpg)
Rose garden

I continued into Tampere center, but around Koskikeskus I turned towards Kangasala. Here it was clear that Tampere is quite a large city. If you avoid the motorway, there's quite a bit of city traffic before you reach Kangasala center. In Kangasala I rode up to the observation tower on Kirkkoharju (Church Ridge). The tower offered a beautiful view over the surrounding lakes.

![Observation tower](kangasala1.jpg)
![View](kangasala2.jpg)

After the view I was hungry, so I found a nice restaurant in Kangasala center. While eating my pizza, I was entertained by watching a fairly large number of motorcycles pass by, including two larger processions of big cruisers. The pizza was very good and the (non-alcoholic) beer suited the occasion.

![Church](kangasala3.jpg)
![Kangasala House](kangasala4.jpg)
Kangasala House

From Kangasala I continued towards Orivesi. For a two-digit road, road 58 is surprisingly narrow and winding. Unfortunately, traffic was quite heavy, so there was no chance to speed up. Orivesi didn't leave a strong impression. The church was a modern, round construction, while the bell tower was built in traditional style. I continued, a short way along highway 9/E63 towards Jyväskylä, but soon turned off towards Juupajoki church village. Juupajoki is one of those municipalities I couldn't have placed before. A small municipality, not really located on any major thoroughfare. Juupajoki's center is Korkeakoski, with an old shoe factory among other things.

There is also a 30 m deep gorge with steep walls eroded during the ice age, [Juupajoki Gorge](https://retkipaikka.fi/juupajoen-rotkon-polut/). Walking down the long stairs was like entering another world. The sound of traffic disappeared and was replaced by the babbling of the brook and birdsong. It got darker. The air smelled of earth and old leaves. At the bottom of the valley is a short nature trail. Well worth a visit. On this day, though, it felt quite warm with motorcycle gear on.

![Juupajoki Gorge](juupajoki1.jpg)
![Troll](juupajoki2.jpg)

After the walk, I continued towards Mänttä. But since I hadn’t seen any military aircraft at Tampere-Pirkkala airfield, I decided to take a detour down to Halli airfield in Kuorevesi, Jämsä. It was several kilometers on gravel, but the road was quite okay and completely dry. Unfortunately, Halli airfield was also deserted, so no Hornets today. I also took a look at Patria’s new factory for F-35 components next to the airfield, but as is often the case, the factory looked boring from the outside. No Lightning II on pedestals or anything like that. However, there was actually a J-35 Draken on display in the center of Kuorevesi. I only noticed it after I had passed by and didn’t feel like turning back – I have some pretty recent photos of a Draken from the Air Force Museum in Tikkakoski.

Now I continued to Mänttä and resumed the planned route. I took a quick look at the Gösta museum. The summer café was closed for the season and the restaurant felt a bit too fancy for my dirty riding gear, so I continued through Vilppula towards Ruovesi.

![Museum Gösta](gosta.jpg)

In Ruovesi I visited Runeberg's spring. I've actually never seen a spring where the water bubbles up like that (the strange light grey area in the picture below). I also tasted the water, it tasted absolutely excellent. Now I felt quite cultural actually. Imagine - me and Finland's national poet [Johan Ludvig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johan_Ludvig_Runeberg) have drunk water from the same spring! I actually couldn't help but sing a couple of stanzas of ["By a spring"](https://runeberg.org/runeberg/1/0132.html), which is said to have been inspired by this very place. And surprisingly, I apparently knew the lyrics for the first three verses completely by heart. Then I went down to Ruovesi harbour where I finally found a place that suited my style. I ate a sugar doughnut and drank coffee in the sun.

![Runeberg's spring](runebergskalla.jpg)

From Ruovesi the journey continued to Kuru. There I continued the Runeberg theme and visited what is called [Fänrik Stål's cottage](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurun_ulkomuseo,_koulumuseo_ja_V%C3%A4nrikki_Stoolin_tupa). An Ensign Polviander, who lived in this cabin supposedly gave Runeberg inspiration for his work [The Tales of Ensign Stål](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tales_of_Ensign_St%C3%A5l).

![The cottage of ensign Stål](kuru.jpg)

Back in the saddle, the journey continued towards [Seitseminen National Park](https://www.luontoon.fi/en/destinations/seitseminen-national-park). Now there was not enough time for a long hike, but a short forest walk was. A short moment of silence by a small lake. Incidentally, hardly any mosquitoes - this would have been a fantastic day for hiking! I even found a bunch of [funnel chanterelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craterellus_tubaeformis), but they were too small to be picked, so I left them to someone else.

![A Lake in Seitseminen National Park](seitseminen1.jpg)
![Mushrooms](seitseminen2.jpg)

Now the shadows began to grow longer and longer. I went back to Parkano, refueled and then went north. I have taken the train many times and marveled at the fact that the train stops at a railway station in the middle of the forest - Parkano. Now I felt like seeing that station, so to speak, from the side of the highway. Sure, it was a few kilometers from the settlement, but it is still not a total wasteland, as it seems from the train window. In any case, it was very peaceful and quiet when I was there.

![Parkano railway station](parkano.jpg)

To save time and get home before dark, I took highway 3/E12 back towards Vaasa. I took a shortcut (shorter but slower) through Koskue, along the old highway. Here, by an old disused shop, there is a [de Havilland Vampire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Havilland_Vampire) airplane on display. I didn't stop, maybe I should have taken a picture. Then I drove on north past Jalasjärvi.

Now the sun was starting to approach the horizon, but I still thought I had time for one last detour. On a hill just before the center of Kurikka is the [Loukajanvuori observation tower](https://retkipaikka.fi/loukajanvuori-nakoalapaikka-kurikassa/). It was not possible to drive all the way up the hill, but it was only a short walk. I climbed the tower and I have to say that for once, an observation tower was unnecessarily high. From the first landing you had a great view over the treetops. And the stairs up there were actually a bit scary.

![Loukajavuori observation tower](loukajavuori0.jpg)
![Wind farm](loukajavuori1.jpg)
![Sunset](loukajavuori2.jpg)

The sun had almost set, so I just had to continue on my way home. I got home after the official sunset time, but with the nice weather it wasn't that dark yet. What's more, it was still really warm and nice for a while after 9 pm. I hadn't even changed into the thicker gloves I had brought. The distance this time was 793 km. I did get a little sore in the back, but I would have liked to have continued for a few more hours if only there had been enough daylight.

All in all, it was a pretty uneventful trip. I didn't talk to anyone all day, except for a few words when I bought coffee. The trip didn't offer any big surprises or any problems. Just great driving all day.

## New municipalities

My long-term goal is to take my motorbike Little Blue to all municipalities in Finland. On this trip, she got to visit a total of 14 new municipalities:

* Parkano
* Ikaalinen
* Ylöjärvi
* Hämeenkyrö
* Nokia
* Vesilahti
* Lempäälä
* Pirkkala
* Tampere
* Kangasala
* Orivesi
* Juupajoki
* Jämsä
* Ruovesi

We have already driven through the outskirts of Mänttä-Vilppula before, but this time we also visited the center.

Little Blue has now visited 161 of Finland's 308 municipalities.
