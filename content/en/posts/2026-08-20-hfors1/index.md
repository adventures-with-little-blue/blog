# On the way to Lahti

A little over two years ago, Little Blue and I made our [first long motorcycle trip](../2024-06-09-helsingfors/) to my employer's summer party in Helsinki. Now it was time for the summer party again and, according to the principle that "Any reason and whatever reason at all" is enough to ride a motorcycle to an event, it became a trip this time as well. This time I optimised the route to and from Helsinki based on the municipalities I still had not visited in Pirkanmaa, Päijät-Häme, Uusimaa and Tavastia Proper. And that added quite a few new names to the list of visited municipalities.

<!--more-->

## The obligatory opening stretch

![Ready to go](01-startklar.jpg "Ready to go. Note how clean and tidy Little Blue is.")

As with all longer trips these days, it began with a transport stretch to reach roads we had not yet travelled. I took the afternoon off to get as far as possible before dusk. The weather was good, and the ride flowed along nicely through Seinäjoki to Alavus.

![Alavus church](02-alavus_kyrka.jpg "Alavus church.")

From Alavus down towards Mänttä-Vilppula we took a new road, fairly narrow and winding. The forecast had promised dry weather, but just south of Alavus there was a proper downpour. When heavy drops started to fall I stopped almost at once by a bus shelter, but as so often before, my clothes still had time to get fairly wet before I got the rain suit on, not soaked through, but wet on the surface. After that there was nothing to do but continue through the pouring rain. I also pulled on my Gore-Tex winter gloves, and this time I made sure not to have the heated grips on. Quite soon the downpour turned into drizzle and more scattered showers.


## Killinkoski

The first new place along the way was the village of Killinkoski in the municipality of Virrat. There has been a ribbon factory in the village since the late nineteenth century. Even today the company Inka Oy manufactures various kinds of straps, ribbons and cords made of textile. Next to the new factory lies the old Killinkoski factory, [Killinkosken Wanha Tehdasmiljöö](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killinkosken_Wanha_tehdasmilj%C3%B6%C3%B6) (fi), now a cultural and museum centre.

![Killinkoski](03-killinkoski.jpg "Killinkoski's old factory.")

![Killinkoski church](04-killinkoski_kyrka.jpg "Killinkoski church.")


## Pohjaslahti, Vilppula and Mänttä

After that the ride continued into the municipality of Mänttä-Vilppula. We have previously ridden through the village of Pohjaslahti, but this time we took the time to ride the gravel road up to the church. Pohjaslahti was formerly an independent municipality. In 1973 two thirds of the municipality were transferred to Vilppula and the rest to Virrat.

![Pohjaslahti church](05-pohjaslahti_kyrka.jpg "Pohjaslahti church.")

A possible destination for the next trip through Pohjaslahti is the observation tower a little east of the village, it looks quite cool.

![Observation tower in Pohjaslahti](06-pohjaslahti_utsiktstorn.jpg "Observation tower in Pohjaslahti.")

The town of Mänttä was created out of the municipality of Vilppula in 1922. In time, Vilppula came to form Mänttä's "rural municipality". In 2009 the two merged again and formed the present town of Mänttä-Vilppula.

![Vilppula church](07-filpula_kyrka.jpg "Vilppula church.")

![Mänttä church](08-mantta_kyrka.jpg "Mänttä church.")

Mänttä has significant paper industry, and the legacy of the former owners, the Serlachius family, is visible in several art museums. Among other things, [the old headquarters of the Serlachius companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustaf_(museum)) is now a museum.

![Serlachius headquarters](09-serlachius_huvudkontor.jpg "Serlachius headquarters.")


## Jämsä

It had stopped raining for a while already. A short distance after the municipal border, in Jämsä, we took a break and I removed the rain suit.

![Rest area](10-rastplats.jpg "Rest area.")

Then the ride continued towards Jämsänkoski. Jämsänkoski formed an independent municipality from 1926, but was reunited with Jämsä into the present town of Jämsä in 2009.

![Jämsänkoski church](11-jamsankoski_kyrka.jpg "Jämsänkoski church.")

We have previously ridden through parts of the town of Jämsä, Halli in the former municipality of Kuorevesi, but had not previously visited the centre. Without Little Blue, however, I have skied at the Himos ski centre just outside central Jämsä.

![Jämsä church](12-jamsa_kyrka.jpg "Jämsä church.")


## Kuhmoinen

Shortly after we crossed the border into the municipality of Kuhmoinen, it started to rain again. I stopped once more at a bus shelter and put the rain suit back on. This time it turned out to be unnecessary. Only a few drops fell, which my Gore-Tex riding clothes can handle perfectly well. Even so, I kept the rain suit on all the way to the evening's destination.

![Rain break](13-regnpaus.jpg "Rain break.")

Kuhmoinen was also the last municipality in the region of Pirkanmaa that we had not visited, so now we have visited all municipalities in Pirkanmaa.

![Kuhmoinen church](15-kuhmois_kyrka.jpg "Kuhmoinen church.")


## Padasjoki

In the municipality of Padasjoki I made a short detour through the village of Arrakoski. I would also gladly have visited the observation towers called the Thomas Towers, (fi: [Tuomastornit](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuomastornit)), but concluded that there was no time for that on this occasion.

![Padasjoki church](16-padasjoki_kyrka.jpg "Padasjoki church.")

## Asikkala

It meant a small detour to ride into the village of Vääksy in the municipality of Asikkala. A steep esker runs through the settlement, which has apparently made straightforward road building more difficult. One road in the centre was closed, so it became quite a long detour. The Vääksy canal is surely worth seeing in sunshine, but on this grey evening the cafés were empty.

![The hill in Vääksy](17-vaaksy_backe.jpg "The hill in Vääksy.")

![Vääksy canal](18-vaaksy_kanal.jpg "Vääksy canal.")

Via Asikkala church village we then continued out to [Pulkkilanharju](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulkkilanharju) (fi), an esker running through Lake Päijänne. The road to Sysmä follows the esker. Even though the road does not run along the very top of the ridge and for most of the way you only see water on one side, it is still quite an experience. Part of Pulkkilanharju belongs to [Päijänne National Park](https://www.luontoon.fi/en/destinations/paijanne-national-park).

![Asikkala church](19-asikkala_kyrka.jpg "Asikkala church.")

![Pulkkilanharju](20-pulkkila1.jpg "A short break on Pulkkilanharju.")

![Pulkkilanharju](21-pulkkila2.jpg "Pulkkilanharju.")

## Sysmä, Hartola and Heinola

From Asikkala we then continued to Sysmä, Hartola and Heinola. There was nothing special to see along the road, but the roads themselves were winding and pleasant, at least as far as Hartola.

![Sysmä church](22-sysma_kyrka.jpg "Sysmä church.")

![Hartola church](23-gustav_adolfs_kyrka.jpg "Hartola church.")

![Heinola rural parish church](24-heinola_sockenkyrka.jpg "Heinola rural parish church.")

![Heinola church](25-heinola_kyrka.jpg "Heinola church.")

In Heinola I bought a bit of dinner, talked to the children on the phone, and then it was motorway riding for the final stretch into Lahti.

## Arriving in Lahti

In Heinola the forecast rain began, and it then continued all the way to the evening's final destination, an Airbnb flat in the Laune district of Lahti. I did not even try to look at sights, but rode straight to the accommodation. It was quarter to ten when I arrived, and it was already fairly dark. Once there, I unloaded my steed, heated the food I had just bought in the microwave, and soon it was time to crawl into bed.

![Overnight stay](27-overnattning.jpg "Overnight stay in an outbuilding.")

![Late dinner](26-sen_middag.jpg "Late dinner.")

## Today's route

![Route](rutt20260820.webp)

My long-term goal is to ride my motorcycle Little Blue through all 308 municipalities of Finland. On a route of 532 km we managed to visit 7 new municipalities today:

* Kuhmoinen (Pirkanmaa)
* Padasjoki (Päijät-Häme)
* Asikkala (Päijät-Häme)
* Sysmä (Päijät-Häme)
* Hartola (Päijät-Häme)
* Heinola (Päijät-Häme)
* Lahti (Päijät-Häme)

With the visit to Kuhmoinen, we have now visited all the municipalities in the region of Pirkanmaa. We also rode through the centre of Jämsä, whereas earlier we had only ridden through a village in the town.
