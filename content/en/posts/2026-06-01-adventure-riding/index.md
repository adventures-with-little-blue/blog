# Adventure riding

Today there was a bit of adventure riding after the evening orienteering. Roads worse than what feels good for the bike.

<!--more-->

First I took a short ride on paved roads before the evening orienteering in Åminne. I enjoyed the sunshine and a temperature just under 20 degrees. Perfect riding weather.

![Orienteers](orienterare.jpg "The orienteers' parking area in Åminne.")

The orienteering went really well this time, with only one mistake, and I managed to sort that out fairly quickly too. For some reason the club Malax IF seems to make easier courses than the club Solf IK does, at least for the C courses, the third level, that is, for us beginners and slower recreational runners. Today there were paths to run along and, above all, terrain features that would catch you if you went past the control. Nice for once not to be the slowest at any single control...

![Bird tower](fageltorn.jpg "The bird tower in Åminne.")

After the orienteering I took the opportunity to make the acquaintance of the bird tower in Åminne. Then I rode home by forest roads. One short stretch of the road was more like a tractor track. Someone had just scraped the road in some way. The result was a surface with patches of loose gravel and sand, mixed with churned-up rather large stones and branches. I rarely ride on this type of road, since most timber truck roads are usually in fairly decent condition. And I realised that this is exactly the kind of road I would definitely like to ride more of, but on a bike better suited to it than Little Blue. Aside from the road tyres, it was also clear that the suspension was not keeping up. And on such an uneven road you would really want to stand up, but Little Blue's geometry simply is not designed for that. I want an enduro bike, or at least an adventure bike!
