# Olavinlinna, Kolovesi and an evening ride

_Day one of our four-day journey to southeast of Finland._

My long-awaited long tour can finally begin. Today Little Blue and I are heading for southeastern Finland. The day's main destination is Olavinlinna Castle in Savonlinna, and the overnight camp is planned for Kolovesi National Park. Will we make it there?

<!--more-->

## Early start

Since the route I planned for today is fairly long, it was important to get moving on time. Almost exactly at 6 in the morning, the outfit rolled out of the yard and off toward the adventure. The first part of every trip goes along roads that are by now fairly familiar. Laihia, Ylistaro, Lapua, Alajärvi. The sun was shining at departure, but once we had passed Lapua, dark clouds began piling up in the east. Roughly at the border with Alajärvi, the sun disappeared behind the thick cloud cover. Contrary to appearances, though, those clouds did not bring any rain, they only blocked the sun.

![Ready for departure](redo_for_avfard.jpg "Little Blue is packed and ready to leave.")

Once the sun disappeared, it also became cooler. In Kyyjärvi I had to conclude that this was not the kind of summer where two layers under the Gore-Tex jacket would be enough, so I had to pull on the fleece as well.

![Kyyjärvi church](kyyjarvis_kyrka2.jpg "Short pause at Kyyjärvi church.")

The route passed Saarijärvi center, so we curved in there. I had previously checked off Saarijärvi as a visited municipality, but then it had actually only been the the village Pylkönmäki, formerly a municipality of its own, that we visited. So this was my first time in the municipal center itself.

![Saarijärvi church](saarijarvis_kyrka.jpg "Saarijärvi church.")

In the same way, Äänekoski center was a new experience, last time we only visited Suolahti. At [Paatela canal](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paatelan_kanava) (fi) I stopped for breakfast with coffee from a thermos and sandwiches brought from home. Then it was time to refuel in Suolahti. I had imagined that this region would have reasonably priced fuel, but it was clearly more expensive here than at the ABC back home.

![Äänekoski church](aanekoskis_kyrka.jpg "Äänekoski church.")

![Paatela canal](paatela.jpg "The lock at Paatela canal.")

![Suolahti church](suolahtis_kyrka.jpg "Suolahti church.")


## Hankasalmi

From Suolahti you head east toward Konnevesi and then turn south toward Hankasalmi. A village with a main street running straight through it. A few shops, a church and a fire station. Nothing special that really sticks in the memory. But Hankasalmi is the first new municipality on this trip and, in fact, the first new municipality of the whole year.

![Hankasalmi church](hankasalmis_kyrka.jpg "Hankasalmi church.")

## Pieksämäki

Pieksämäki is a somewhat larger place. The [old church](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pieks%C3%A4m%C3%A4en_Vanha_kirkko) (fi) stands beautifully on a hill a little outside the city center. The center itself is built around the railway, and much still seems to revolve around it. There were some people and some activity in the market square, which isn't bad for a Monday late morning. The city's Prisma is, at least in part, housed in rebuilt old locomotive sheds, which is rather special. I skipped various railway-related tourist attractions, such as the railway museum, and continued on toward Varkaus.

![Pieksämäki old church](pieksamakis_gamla_kyrka.jpg "Pieksämäki old church.")


## Lunch in Varkaus

If Pieksämäki is built around the railway, then Varkaus is built around its paper mill. Stora Enso's factory sits right in the middle of town on what might otherwise have been the city's best plots. The main shopping street is one-way for quite a long stretch and actually rather pleasant. There are shops, restaurants, a cinema and other things here. You could say that this town really does have a style all its own. I had lunch at the restaurant "Kaks Ruusua", which seemed quite nice. It probably has a history connected to the city's industrial heritage.

![Restaurant Kaks Ruusua](kaks_ruusua2.jpg "Restaurant Kaks Ruusua.")
![Lunch](kaks_ruusua1.jpg "Lunch in stylish surroundings.")

![Varkaus church](varkaus_kyrka2.jpg "Varkaus main church.")


## Joroinen

Just south of Varkaus lies Joroinen. Joroinen is known for its many manor houses. I made a point of riding past one of them, [Järvikylä Manor](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%A4rvikyl%C3%A4,_Jorois) (sv). If the name sounds familiar, it may be because a large share of the potted herbs sold in Finland are grown here. Their outbuildings even appear on the labels, if I remember correctly.

![Järvikylä](jarvikylan_kartano.jpg "An outbuilding at Järvikylä manor, known from the fresh salads and herbs sold in grocery stores.")

I also rode past Hotel Joronjälki, the hotel that was hit by an explosion accident in November 2025. Gas cylinders exploded in a car parked in the yard. Two people died and the hotel was badly damaged. I was not enough of a disaster tourist to turn in to the hotel, but it did seem to still have activity, as there were quite a few cars parked in the yard.

![Joroinen church](jorois_kyrka.jpg "Joroinen church.")

Just outside the center of Joroinen lies [Varkaus Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varkaus_Airport). The airport no longer has scheduled passenger traffic and is no longer owned by Finavia. Instead, Joroinen municipality uses the terminal, which looks fresh and fairly new, for municipal administration. At the same time, people in Varkaus and Joroinen are working to bring passenger traffic back to the airfield.

![Varkaus airport terminal](varkaus_flygstation.jpg "The former Varkaus airport terminal is now used by Joroinen municipality.")


## Rantasalmi

After driving almost all the time on broad and straight roads, the road from Joroinen to Rantasalmi offered a little more variation. Long sweeping curves. But unfortunately also quite a lot of traffic. The center of Rantasalmi looked much like many other town centers. The famous architect [Eliel Saarinen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliel_Saarinen) was apparently born here, but I noticed no tourist traps connected to that. In any case the church is large and stately, grander than the size of the municipality would suggest.

![Rantasalmi church](randasalmis_kyrka.jpg "Rantasalmi church.")


## Savonlinna and Olavinlinna

After Rantasalmi I approached Savonlinna, and it was immediately noticeable that I was getting closer to a somewhat larger town and, above all, a larger tourist destination. The main road, highway 14, into town was fairly full of cars, motorhomes and caravans. In town itself, traffic was quite lively in the middle of the day. A few streets were closed, so I had to alter the route slightly, but it's hardly a city where you are likely to get badly lost.

![Savonlinna Cathedral](nyslotts_domkyrka.jpg "Savonlinna Cathedral, which is no longer actually a cathedral.")

The day's primary destination was [Olavinlinna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olavinlinna), which can certainly be counted among Finland's best-known buildings. Even though the castle receives huge numbers of tourists, there is no dedicated parking area, so you have to park on the street. Oddly enough, I managed to find a spot practically as close as you can get, and without any parking fee besides.

![Parking on the street](olofsborg1.jpg "There is no separate parking area near Olavinlinna, but there is free parking along the streets.")

It really is an impressive place. I joined a guided tour to see as much of the castle as possible. We were close to a hundred people, but fortunately we were divided into two groups. The tour lasted about an hour, and a large part of the time was spent waiting for everyone to make their way up and down narrow stairs and passages.

![Olavinlinna](olofsborg3.jpg "Olavinlinna, perhaps the most classic view of them all.")

![Olavinlinna](olofsborg2.jpg "Two of the three remaining towers. Two towers have been destroyed.")

I didn't make any special haste, but enjoyed walking around in the historic setting. But when I eventually came out of the castle, I realised that I was well behind my preliminary timetable. I had intended to sit down in a restaurant and eat a proper dinner, but instead decided to drag myself back to the motorcycle and continue onward. I filled the tank, bought a ready-made salad at the shop and then continued north. I also made a short detour via [Savonlinna Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savonlinna_Airport), which lies right beside the road to Enonkoski.

![Savonlinna Airport](nyslotts_flygplats.jpg "Savonlinna Airport. No crowding here.")


## Enonkoski and Kolovesi National Park

Enonkoski was not a large place. It has nevertheless been on my radar before because I have been planning an [Iron Butt SS1000 "Name on the map"](https://www.ibafinland.fi/jasenille/ajosuoritukset/) (fi). In that ride you are supposed to visit and refuel in places whose initial letters match all the letters in your full name, for example "E" for Enonkoski. I could now see with my own eyes that there is some kind of fuel station in Enonkoski, so refueling here should be possible.

![Enonkoski church](enonkoskis_kyrka.jpg "Enonkoski church.")

After the church village I continued north to [Hanhivirta ferry](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanhivirtaf%C3%A4rjan) (sv). I had truly lucky timing. On the way toward the ferry landing I noticed that there was no oncoming traffic at all, so I guessed the ferry had not yet arrived. Almost the same moment I got the ferry landing in sight, the ferry came in. I didn't even need to shut off the engine, only wait a few seconds before I could roll aboard. The ferry became almost full, so once I, as the last vehicle, had rolled on, it set off again immediately.

![Hanhivirta ferry](hanhivirta_farja.jpg "Hanhivirta ferry, almost full.")

From the ferry it was then a stretch of slow riding along a poor road behind all the cars from the ferry. After 15 km I turned down toward Kirkkoranta campsite in [Kolovesi National Park](https://www.luontoon.fi/en/destinations/kolovesi-national-park), the night's camp. There was plenty of room in the parking area, but I had to carry the tent and equipment a good hundred meters into the forest to the actual camping area. There I quickly pitched the tent and then enjoyed a dinner consisting of salad, mineral water and the last sandwiches I had brought with me. The food was so-so, but the place was enchanting. Beautiful and quiet, rest for the soul and the ears.

![Tent pitched](kolovesi1.jpg "The tent pitched in Kolovesi National Park.")

![Dinner](kolovesi2.jpg "Simple dinner in fantastic surroundings.")


## An extra evening ride

You might perhaps think that 570 km would have been enough for the first day, but there were a couple of things to keep in mind. First, by skipping dinner in Savonlinna and being fairly quick about pitching the tent, I had saved a little time and was now slightly ahead of schedule. Second, I had planned a small extra ride for the evening, just in case. The ride was meant to take me through a few municipalities that had ended up a little off to the side during the previous trip and thus had not yet been visited. So before long I was back in the saddle again, this time with a lighter load since the saddlebags and tent were left in Kolovesi.


## Heinävesi

First up was the center of Heinävesi. I rode through Heinävesi on last year's [rainy eastward tour](../2025-07-08-osterled2/), but then I visited New Valamo Monastery, not the municipal center. It wasn't particularly spectacular, a typical small place with a church on a hill at the edge of the village.

![Heinävesi church](heinavesis_kyrka.jpg "Heinävesi church.")

More spectacular was the next stop. After circling Lake Kermajärvi, that turned into almost 30 km, and passing [Karvio Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karvio_Canal), where Neste Karvionkanava is a well-known stopping place, I came to [Varistaipale Canal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varistaipale_Canal). This is apparently the country's only canal with as many as four lock steps. The total drop is 13-14 meters. Quite exciting. Unfortunately there was no traffic through the canal at this hour.

![Varistaipale Canal](varistaipale_kanal.jpg "Varistaipale Canal with no less than four steps.")

I also made a short detour from the route to [Lintula Monastery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lintula_Holy_Trinity_Convent), Finland's only Orthodox nunnery. A woman with a small child was picking berries outside the monastery, but otherwise it was fairly quiet. I suspected the nuns did not want visitors after 8 PM, so I rode on.

![Lintula Monastery](lintula.jpg "Lintula Monastery, Finland's only Orthodox nunnery.")


## Tuusniemi, Kaavi, Polvijärvi and Outokumpu

After that Little Blue and I headed for municipalities we had not visited before. We rode through Tuusniemi and Kaavi. I was almost beginning to regret my decision to go for an evening ride. The weather was still overcast and gray, so it was starting to feel a little cold. The distances were longer than I had expected, the road from Lintula to Tuusniemi alone was almost 40 km. But the worst part was that the roads were very dull and in rather poor condition. Between the municipal centers there was not much to see, mostly forest. A few houses here and there, perhaps a lake glimpsed through the trees. But no spectacular views, high hills or anything like that. And the municipal centers were small and dull. What saved the whole thing was probably that the road between Kaavi and Polvijärvi was suitably winding, at least almost smooth enough, and traffic was very sparse. It was enjoyable riding.

![Tuusniemi church](tuusniemis_kyrka.jpg "Tuusniemi church.")

![Kaavi church](kaavis_kyrka.jpg "Kaavi church. The old one burned down.")

![Polvijärvi church](polvijarvis_kyrka1.jpg "Polvijärvi church.")

In Polvijärvi it had become chilly enough that I had to pull on my fleece and thicker gloves again. Here I also passed a memorial standing outside the municipal hall. The monument commemorates the [lowering of Lake Höytiäinen](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/H%C3%B6yti%C3%A4isen_j%C3%A4rvenlasku) (fi) in 1859.

![Monument](polvijarvi_monument.jpg "Monument commemorating the lowering of Lake Höytiäinen.")

Lake Höytiäinen is a large lake lying between Polvijärvi and Kontiolahti, directly north of Joensuu. The idea was to lower the water level a little in order to expose land for cultivation by diverting water through a new canal, but the dams gave way and enormous masses of water rushed out toward Pyhäselkä. In two weeks the water level of the lake dropped by 7.5 meters, and later by another 2 meters, as 3 trillion liters of water ran out. This caused flooding downstream as far as Lappeenranta. For Polvijärvi this was a transformative event: the landscape changed dramatically as the shoreline moved and large areas of new land and farmland were exposed along the lake. That is how things can sometimes turn out.

The road back to Kolovesi was, if possible, even duller. The center of Outokumpu was almost deserted at 10 PM, and the church is on the ugly side. I had not noticed that there is a much more interesting church in Kuusjärvi, almost on the route back, so unfortunately I missed that one.

![Outokumpu church](outokumpus_kyrka.jpg "Outokumpu church.")

Instead I continued southward along the dull and miserably poor roads. Part of the route was actually gravel, which I had forgotten. That added a small extra challenge in the deepening dusk. By this stage the long hours of sitting were definitely beginning to be felt in my backside, so the last stretch was purely transport, naturally with a constant lookout for wild animals in the dusk. Not nearly as enjoyable as riding otherwise is.

## "Home" again

When I got "home" to Kolovesi again the time was 11:20 PM and it was beginning to get dark. Now it was just a matter of brushing my teeth and crawling into the sleeping bag. I set the alarm for 6:00 AM. I suspect it didn't take more than a quarter of an hour from parking the bike until I was asleep.

![Night in Kolovesi](kolovesi3.jpg "Night in Kolovesi National Park.")

![Good night](kolovesi4.jpg "At last the sleeping bag. Good night!")


## Today's route

It became a rather long first day, Blue's odometer showed a total of 879 km, including the extra evening route. As previously noted, I started feeling it in my backside after 800 km, which lines up with my earlier experiences as well. Otherwise everything went well.

![Route](dag1-rutt.webp "Today's route.")

* Pieksämäki (Southern Savonia)
* Varkaus (Northern Savonia)
* Joroinen (Northern Savonia)
* Rantasalmi (Southern Savonia)

* Enonkoski (Southern Savonia)
* Tuusniemi (Northern Savonia)
* Kaavi (Northern Savonia)
