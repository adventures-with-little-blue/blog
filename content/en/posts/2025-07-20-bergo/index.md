## Try a Beach: Bredhällan Beach

The “Try a Beach” series continues. We head out with the bike and take a swim. This time we’re testing Bredhällan Beach on Bergö.

<!--more-->

### How to get there

Bredhällan is on Bergö, and Bergö is, well, an island. There’s no bridge, but there is a road ferry. That’s kind of fun — I had actually never taken Blue on a road ferry before. Nor had I visited Bergö since the new and much bigger ferry [Vikare](https://ksloy.fi/en/lauttaliikenne) arrived. It’s truly a beast.

The road to the ferry isn’t great. First Strandvägen to Molpe, then a straight, boring road with bad tarmac from Molpe to the ferry dock. And of course, you have to take the same boring route home — there’s only one road to choose.

![Waiting for the ferry](farja1.jpg)

Once on Bergö, just follow the signs to the beach. It’s surprisingly far, you’d think it’s a small island, but it takes a while to ride.

![Arch at the beach](strand1.jpg)

### Beach rating

Here you’ll find pretty much everything you could need at a beach. Changing rooms (+), toilet (+). Grill area (+) and picnic tables (+). Trash bins (+). And just a short walk away, a kiosk (+).

There’s also a playground, swings, canoe rental, mini golf (+). And actually a campsite next door.

The sandy part of the beach is large enough (+), the sand goes out to the floating dock (+). There’s also a diving tower (+). The water depth at the floating dock isn’t enough for jumping in, but you can start your swim from the ladder.
![The beach](strand2.jpg)

The water was relatively clear (+) compared to most beaches in Ostrobothnia.

*Summary:* Really good beach with everything you need. The only minus is the boring road and waiting for the ferry.

*Recommendation:* Go here to swim, chill, or hang out with friends.

*Rating:* 3.5/5

### Conclusion

I would have liked to stay longer, but got a bit stressed about the ferry. I did take a ride via Österändan and Perisgrund. The latter is a spot on the shore without protective islands outside. It felt different, much more barren than the rest of Bergö.

![On the ferry again](farja2.jpg)

On the way home from Bergö, I took a detour out to Bockören to enjoy the sunset.

![Bockören](bockoren.jpg)

Today's trip was 99 km long.
