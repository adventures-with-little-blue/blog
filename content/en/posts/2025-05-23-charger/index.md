# The Solution to the Charging Problems?

All spring, Little Blue has been acting up. The battery has slowly discharged while riding, causing the heated grips to turn off and eventually the engine to run poorly. A few hours with the CTek charger and everything would be fine again – for a few hundred kilometers. As a side effect, I also managed to kill a perfectly healthy battery by deep discharging it beyond recovery when I left the power on overnight.

<!--more-->

With a multimeter, I concluded the problem must be the stator. One of the three wires had no contact at all, one had way too high resistance, and only one was even close to the specified values. The rectifier/regulator, on the other hand, seemed fine according to the meter. So I bought a refurbished stator, delivered by mail from the UK, costing about €100. Sigh, motorcycles are expensive. I could have swallowed that expense, if only – the problem wasn't the stator at all.

Today I started the project of replacing the stator. I began by disconnecting the connector and... wait a minute. If the connector looks like this, it can't possibly work!

![Broken connector](broken.jpg)

Out came the soldering iron. A bit of solder later and all three poles on the old stator showed acceptable resistance (not exactly to spec, but close enough). So, apply some electrical tape, reconnect the connector, and it was ready to ride again.

![Soldered wires](fixing.jpg)

![Acceptable resistance](measure.jpg)

![Fixed connector plugged in](fixed.jpg)

What can you learn from this? First, I can't understand how I managed to ride nearly 500 km on a motorcycle where only one of the three stator poles was connected – and with the heated grips on. Did the rectifier/regulator still manage to charge the battery?

Perhaps one should take things apart and inspect them properly before ordering expensive spare parts? Maybe, but at the same time, the bike was running – and I didn't want to be without a bike while waiting for parts. The only sensible conclusion is that I need two bikes (at least), in order to have one to ride while the other one is in the shop. Now I just have to sell that idea to the family...
