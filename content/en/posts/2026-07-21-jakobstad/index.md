# The Fäboda-Pörkenäs Road

My daughter had a football match in Pedersöre. This time I was not acting as chauffeur, but decided instead to ride there on the bike and cheer her on. To keep it from being boring, I did not choose the most direct road, neither on the way there nor on the way back. For a long time I had wanted to ride the road from Jakobstad via Fäboda and Pörkenäs out to Grisselören in Nykarleby. Now I had the chance to do it.

<!--more-->

The afternoon was sunny when I set off. I rode along the Kyrö River to Lillkyro, then on to Vörå and from there toward Ylihärmä. I took the opportunity to photograph the churches in Ylihärmä and Alahärmä before continuing toward Kortesjärvi and Evijärvi.

![Ylihärmä church](yliharma_kyrka.jpg "Ylihärmä church")

![Alahärmä church](alaharma_kyrka.jpg "Alahärmä church")

![Kortesjärvi church](kortesjarvi_kyrka.jpg "Kortesjärvi church")

In Evijärvi there is a monument in the yard in front of the municipal hall, the "Memorial to the war mobilization" (Sotaan lähdön muistomerkki), which includes, among other things, a light field howitzer. It is a reminder that war really has affected everything and everyone in this country, including us here in the western part of Finland.

![Memorial](evijarvi_haubits.jpg "Memorial to the war mobilization 1939-1944.")

I also photographed Evijärvi church and then continued via Esse and Esse church toward the football field in Kållby. I had never ridden that stretch of road before. But it was rather dull. Mostly just forest.

![Evijärvi church](evijarvi_kyrka.jpg "Evijärvi church")

![Esse church](esse_kyrka.jpg "Esse church")

After the match I went to fill up the tank, and then continued the ride with Pedersöre church and Jakobstad church.

![Pedersöre church](pedersore_kyrka.jpg "Pedersöre church")

![Pedersöre poor box figure](pedersore_fattiggubbe.jpg "Pedersöre church's poor box figure looks a little out of place there beside the impressively large church gates")

![Jakobstad church](jakobstads_kyrka.jpg "Jakobstad church")

After that I headed out toward Fäboda. It has been many years since I last took this road, and it may even be that I have never driven it myself before, not even by car. At any rate it did not quite look the way I remembered, but that may be because quite a lot of houses have been built along the road during the past 25 years. I arrived at Fäboda coffee house just before closing time. I bought an ice cream so I would not have to drink coffee out of a paper cup.

![Fäboda](faboda1.jpg "The parking area by Fäboda coffee house.")

![Ice cream](faboda3.jpg "Ice cream!")

![Fäboda beach](faboda2.jpg "Fäboda beach.")

Unfortunately it had now become cloudy and no longer as warm. Otherwise I would have brought swim trunks and a towel for a short evening dip, but now I let it be. After enjoying my ice cream, I continued on toward Pörkenäs. Here the poor asphalt gave way to an equally bumpy gravel road. There were people at the camp center, so I turned around in the parking area. Otherwise I would gladly have taken a short walk and revived memories of the many happenings there. Instead I stopped a bit farther on at a place with a view out over the sea.

![Pörkenäs](porkenas1.jpg "Pörkenäs.")

![Pörkenäs](porkenas2.jpg "Pörkenäs.")

Then I continued along the little gravel road toward Grisselören. I had certainly never ridden that road before. The road itself was rather dull, with not much to see. The harbor at Grisselören was very nice, with lots of small summer cottages and boat sheds beside the marina. Out by the service building there seemed to be fish for sale on a self-service basis, but I did not look into the freezers.

![Grisselören](grisseloren.jpg "Grisselören.")

From Grisselören I continued along the paved road toward Soklot and then into central Nykarleby. I photographed the church and Brostugan. But the bridge was closed for repairs, so I could not get across.

![Nykarleby church](nykarleby_kyrka.jpg "Nykarleby kyrka.")

![Nykarleby Brostugan](nykarleby_brostugan.jpg "Summer Café Brostugan, has server coffee for more than 100 years.")

I also stopped at Munsala church. It is quite beautiful, a little different by Ostrobothnian standards. It had already turned a little chilly, so it was time to pull out the fleece and the thicker gloves.

![Munsala bell tower](munsala_klockstapel.jpg "Munsala church's bell tower.")

![Munsala church](munsala_kyrka.jpg "Munsala church.")

As it was now starting to get dark, I took the quickest and dullest road straight home. I got home a few minutes before midnight. The ride came to 323 km.
