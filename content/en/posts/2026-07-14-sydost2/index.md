# Borderlands, Punkaharju, Lappeenranta and southern Saimaa

_Day two of our four-day journey to southeast of Finland._


The plan for day two of our southeast tour is to really reach what you can start calling southeastern Finland, all the way to Lappeenranta. The day's anticipated highlights are Kerimäki church, the Punkaharju ridge, the Simo Häyhä Museum and Lappeenranta Fortress. Which overnight stop it will be depends on how the trip progresses, there are two options.

<!--more-->

Even though I got into my sleeping bag late yesterday, I still managed to drag myself up fairly soon after the phone alarm went off at 06:00. It was fairly quick work to pack everything up, carry the luggage to the parking area and lash it all onto Little Blue. Only once the saddlebags were already in place did it occur to me that the chain would probably appreciate some lubrication after yesterday's nearly 900 km, but I couldn't be bothered to tear the saddlebags off again, so I just climbed into the saddle. I was on the road around 7:10, which I think was pretty good going.

![Ready](redo.jpg "It's a little after seven, and Blue and I are ready for today's ordeals.")

## Liperi and Rääkkylä

First the ride headed north along the same road as the day before. We rode through the center of Liperi. We have passed through this municipality before along the main road, but now we got to see the center as well. There were already some people up and about a little before 8 o'clock.

![Liperi church](libelits_kyrka.jpg "Liperi church.")

From Liperi we continued toward Rääkkylä. On the way you take a road ferry across [Arvinsalmi](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arvinsalmif%C3%A4rjan) (sv). When I reached the ferry landing, the ferry had just left from the other side, so I had to wait a few minutes. And there were not exactly many vehicles aboard [Nestori](https://ksloy.fi/en/lauttaliikenne), one of Finland's largest cable ferries. Nestori is incidentally a sister ship and almost identical to Vikare, which runs between Bredskäret in Korsnäs and Bergö in Malax, and we have taken that one [before](../2025-07-20-bergo/).

![Arvinsalmi ferry Nestori](arvinsalmis_farja1.jpg "The Arvinsalmi ferry Nestori is a sister ship of the Bergö ferry Vikare.")

![Arvinsalmi ferry](arvinsalmis_farja2.jpg "No crowding aboard Nestori.")

Rääkkylä church village was not particularly large, but the church stood beautifully on top of a hill. Below the church a farmer was harvesting grass with an old-fashioned small forage harvester and an ordinary single-axle grain trailer. I thought such small rigs belonged to history, these days you mostly see huge self-propelled harvesting monsters followed by a whole line of enormous tractors with specialized silage wagons.

![Rääkkylä church](raakkylas_kyrka.jpg "Rääkkylä church.")

## Tohmajärvi and Kitee

The road to the next target, Tohmajärvi, followed narrow and winding gravel roads. At this point I started to realize that a gravel road in properly hilly terrain in eastern Finland is considerably more challenging to ride than the gravel roads of the flatlands I am used to. In some places heavy rain had run along the road and carved fairly deep erosion ruts. Riding down into one of those could very well lead to a crash. So progress was fairly slow. As a consolation prize, though, we at least got to ride past some really beautiful lakes, and in a few places the road crossed high hills with fine views over the surrounding landscape.

In Tohmajärvi I was struck by how proper and well-kept everything seemed. I had expected a small sleepy place with broken asphalt and old closed village shops. Instead I was met by broad newly paved roads, cycle paths, sports facilities and well-kept detached houses. There may not have been all that much to see in the village itself after all. Instead I rode out to the church, which lies a few kilometres outside the center on the shore of Lake Tohmajärvi. Very beautiful and peaceful. I made morning coffee there in the parking area. I also managed to fill my empty water bottles down by the church service building. Much appreciated.

![Tohmajärvi church](tohmajarvis_kyrka2.jpg "Made morning coffee by Tohmajärvi church. Quiet and peaceful.")

From Tohmajärvi church we continued via [Saarios hydroelectric power station](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarion_voimalaitos) (fi). There is a museum there, but it was not open and there was not much to see from the outside. Then on to the village of Värtsilä, formerly a municipality of its own.

![Värtsilä church](vartsilas_kyrka.jpg "Värtsilä church.")

The greater part of Värtsilä, especially all the industry, ended up on the other side of the border when the war ended in 1944. And the border is probably what still defines the place today. The newly built border fence is clearly visible from the road, the border zone begins right at the roadside ditch. The Niirala border crossing is closed and blocked off with barbed wire. The road sign says it is only 66 km to [Sortavala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sortavala), the old Finnish town on the shore of Lake Ladoga, and yet it feels unimaginably far away.

![Border fence](gransstangsel.jpg "Part of the newly built border fence.")

![Niirala](niirala.jpg "The Niirala border crossing is closed. With barbed wire and all.")

From Värtsilä we continued to Kitee. The band [Nightwish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nightwish) was founded in Kitee, and there is apparently a Nightwish museum here. Unfortunately the opening hours did not allow for a visit, and I did not catch even the slightest glimpse of advertising signs or anything of the sort, despite riding past where the museum ought to be. All in all there was not that much to see in the center of Kitee. We continued through Puhos toward Kerimäki.

![Kitee church](kides_kyrka.jpg "Kitee church.")

## Kerimäki and Punkaharju

The next stop was Kerimäki, formerly a municipality of its own but nowadays part of Savonlinna. I have in fact visited [Kerimäki church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerim%C3%A4ki_Church) before, but that was perhaps 25 years ago, so I had been looking forward to another visit. Unfortunately the entire church, bell tower and all, was closed for renovation. I had admittedly read that something like this was in the works, but I had still thought one might be able to see something other than a building wrapped in plastic. But that was not the case. Instead I bought expensive petrol at the ABC and had a short conversation with a woman in the S-Market parking lot whose husband rode a motorcycle. She had just been involved in a dangerous overtaking situation in which a motorcycle had almost been hit by a car.

![Kerimäki church](kerimakis_kyrka.jpg "Kerimäki church, wrapped in plastic.")

After stocking up on a few fresh supplies, I continued the short distance to the Punkaharju ridge, where I sat down to enjoy the view and some sort of lunch, consisting of coffee from the thermos that I had made in Tohmajärvi and [lihalörtsy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%C3%B6rtsy), a kind of meat pastry from the Savonlinna region.

![Punkaharju](punkaharju1.jpg "Parking spot along the Punkaharju ridge.")

![Punkaharju](punkaharju2.jpg "Lunch break in Punkaharju. This meat-filled pastry is called lihalörtsy.")

![Punkaharju church](punkaharjus_kyrka.jpg "Punkaharju church.")

## Parikkala, Rautjärvi and Ruokolahti

From Punkaharju we continued through the center of Parikkala. A railway runs through the village and splits the built-up area into two parts, and there do not seem to be many places where you can get from one side to the other, at least not by car. Otherwise I did not notice anything particularly special. When you drive long distances through many localities, they all start to resemble one another after a while. Mostly low houses, a few shops, a fire station, perhaps a market square. Parikkala church lies a short distance outside the center in what is called the church village.

![Parikkala church](parikkalas_kyrka.jpg "Parikkala church.")

Back out on the main road, route 6 southward, there is one of the few places where the Russian border can be seen from a major road. At Joukinsalmi it is only a few hundred metres to the border. From there on to the next village. Simpele used to be a municipality of its own but was merged with the neighbouring municipality of Rautjärvi in 1973. Despite the name, Simpele is the center of the merged municipality, while the villages in the old Rautjärvi municipality are considerably smaller. Both old Simpele and old Rautjärvi lost large areas to the Russians in 1940.

![Simpele church](simpeles_kyrka.jpg "Simpele church.")

After Simpele we headed out onto the main road again and then turned onto road 3991 toward the village of Miettilä, the center of the old Rautjärvi municipality until 1973. This road was actually one of the highlights of the day. Winding, lightly trafficked and with sufficiently good asphalt, it offered 14 km of genuinely enjoyable riding all the way to the Kollaa and Simo Häyhä Museum.

![Museum](hayha_museum1.jpg "The Kollaa and Simo Häyhä Museum.")

The Kollaa and Simo Häyhä Museum tells partly about the [Battle of Kollaa in 1939-1940](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kollaa), ([which held out](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kollaa_kest%C3%A4%C3%A4) (fi)), during the Winter War, and partly about [Simo Häyhä](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simo_H%C3%A4yh%C3%A4), known as one of the most effective snipers in world history. It was certainly interesting, but the number of physical objects was actually quite small. What you got the most out of were probably the texts, much of which is surely available on the internet as well.

![Medal](hayha_museum2.jpg "Simo Häyhä's medal.")

From the museum I rode on to Rautjärvi church. The old church burned down on Christmas Day 2022, in what is suspected to have been an arson attack. The stone foundation of the old church is still there. The new church was built right beside it, beautifully situated on the shore of Lake Rautjärvi.

![Rautjärvi church](rautjarvis_kyrka.jpg "To the right of the bell tower you can glimpse the new Rautjärvi church. To the left is the foundation of the old church.")

I also took the opportunity to make use of the facilities in the modern service building. Then it was on toward Ruokolahti. I should probably admit that I took an extra detour via Ruokolahti just to visit every place, since the municipal center otherwise lies a little off the road toward Imatra and Lappeenranta. And had I not taken that extra detour, I would have missed the fantastically fine bell tower by Ruokolahti church.

![Ruokolahti church bell tower](ruokolax_kyrka1.jpg "The bell tower of Ruokolahti church is something out of the ordinary.")

![Ruokolahti church](ruokolax_kyrka2.jpg "Ruokolahti church.")

## Imatra

National road 6 runs straight through Imatra, but instead I followed smaller roads into the town, which brought me to [The Church of the Three Crosses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_the_Three_Crosses). This is part of the [Aalto Works](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalto_Works) ensemble on [UNESCO's World Heritage List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Heritage_Site). Unfortunately the church is in poor condition and currently out of use.

![The Church of the Three Crosses](imatra_tre_korsens_kyrka.jpg "The Church of the Three Crosses in Imatra. Designed by Alvar Aalto.")

I was now feeling a little pressed for time, so I rode more or less straight through Imatra. I did cross the Vuoksi at Imatrankoski, but I did not stop to stroll around. I only quickly took a photo of the legendary [Imatra State Hotel](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imatra_Statshotell) (sv) and then continued in the direction of Lappeenranta.

![Tainionkoski church](imatra_tainionkoskis_kyrka.jpg "Tainionkoski church, Imatra")

![Imatra State Hotel](imatra_stadshotell.jpg "The iconic Imatra State Hotel.")

## Lappeenranta

To avoid having to ride the boring motorway for too long, I turned up toward Joutseno and admired the church and the water tower. Joutseno was previously a municipality of its own and later a town, but is now part of Lappeenranta.

![Joutseno church](joutsenos_kyrka.jpg "Joutseno church.")

![Joutseno water tower](joutsenos_vattentorn.jpg "Joutseno water tower.")

After Joutseno we headed back out onto the motorway again and made straight for the center of Lappeenranta and [Lappeenranta Fortress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lappeenranta_Fortress). I walked around the area for a while.

![Lappee Maria church](villmanstrand_lappee_maria_kyrka.jpg "Lappee Maria church in central Lappeenranta.")

![Lappeenranta Fortress](villmanstrand1.jpg "Lappeenranta Fortress.")

![Lappeenranta Fortress](villmanstrand2.jpg "Lappeenranta Fortress.")

![Pokrova church](villmanstrand_pokrovkyrkan.jpg "The Orthodox Pokrova church in Lappeenranta Fortress.")

By now I was feeling hungry, so I rolled a short distance down toward the waterfront to Bistro Wanha Makasiini, just below the fortress walls. This became the day's hot meal and a genuinely relaxing moment in the sun. After the meal I took a short walk to the annual sandcastle event and looked at the creations there. It turned out that they cheat a little, they mix wood glue into the sand. That is why the shapes hold for several months.

![Dinner](villmanstrand3.jpg "Dinner at Bistro Wanha Makasiini.")

![Sandcastle](villmanstrand4.jpg "Sandcastle.")

Now it was also time to decide where we should aim to find overnight accommodation. One option was the campsite just outside the town. The other option meant nearly 200 km more riding. I felt in good shape, the clouds had given way to an almost cloudless sky and the time was only 18:00, so I decided to continue. We went to refuel and then rode via the [airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lappeenranta_Airport) toward the next municipality, Lemi.

![Lappeenranta airport](villmanstrands_flygplats.jpg "Lappeenranta airport.")


## Around the southeastern part of Saimaa

Lemi is a small municipality. Their big thing seems to be the special dish [särä](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A4r%C3%A4) (fi), a kind of stew of lamb and potatoes. It would have been fun to try it, but it is not the kind of dish you just walk in and order from the menu. You have to book a table well in advance, which does not fit especially well with a motorcycle trip on an uncertain timetable.

![Lemi church](lemis_kyrka.jpg "Lemi church.")

From Lemi we continued south toward the municipality of Luumäki, once again along fine winding roads. In Luumäki lies [Kotkaniemi](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kotkaniemi) (fi), the home of President [P. E. Svinhufvud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._E._Svinhufvud) for many years. That would certainly have been worth a visit, but naturally it was closed at that time of the evening.

![Kotkaniemi](kotkaniemi.jpg "Kotkaniemi. This was President Svinhufvud's home for many years.")

I then rode via Luumäki church to the municipal center of Taavetti, but I did not feel I had time to take a walk up to [Taavetti Fortress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taavetti_Fortress), so instead we continued north toward Savitaipale.

![Luumäki church](luumakis_kyrka.jpg "Luumäki church.")

Savitaipale did not leave any particularly strong impression. A small municipality, though quite pleasant-looking all the same. We continued toward Taipalsaari, and with that we also left national road 13 toward Mikkeli and instead headed out along narrow twisting back roads. This was in fact the best riding of the day, roughly 25 km from Savitaipale church to Taipalsaari church.

![Savitaipale church](savitaipales_kyrka.jpg "Savitaipale church.")

## Taipalsaari and overnight camp at Päihäniemi recreation area

![Taipalsaari church](taipalsaaris_kyrka.jpg "Taipalsaari church.")

From Taipalsaari church in the municipal center it was another good 20 km to the Päihäniemi recreation area. We arrived at 21:00, and the areas intended for camping were already almost full, even though this is not an official campsite. There were mostly motorhomes there, but also a few tents and someone who apparently was spending the night in a passenger car. I pitched my tent among all the others and then went for a swim in Saimaa. Quite warm and pleasant, even though the sun had disappeared behind the trees.

![The tent at Päihäniemi](paihaniemi1.jpg "The tent is up and Little Blue gets to stand beside it.")

After the swim I put my tank bag on my back and went for a short walk along the nature trail. At the same time I ate a little evening meal while admiring the view from the ridge. Then I crawled into my sleeping bag, for once already before 23:00. Unfortunately the motorhome neighbours had their television turned up quite loud, there was a World Cup match on, so it took a while to get hold of sleep. Well then, just wait, tomorrow I am starting the bike at 07:00...

![Päihäniemi](paihaniemi2.jpg "Evening snack up on the ridge, along the nature trail.")

![Päihäniemi](paihaniemi3.jpg "Dusk over the shore at Päihäniemi.")

![Päihäniemi](paihaniemi4.jpg "Dusk over the shore at Päihäniemi.")


## The day's route

Today the route turned out to be a fairly suitable length, 628 km. It was another long day, certainly, but both body and mind are still holding up, and so is the motorcycle. I would gladly have spent a little more time in Imatra, and it does annoy me a little that Kerimäki church was closed, but otherwise it feels like a successful day.

![Route](dag2-rutt.webp "The day's route.")

My long-term goal is to ride my motorcycle Little Blue through all 308 municipalities of Finland. On this day I added 12 new municipalities to the list of those visited:

* Rääkkylä (North Karelia)
* Tohmajärvi (North Karelia)
* Kitee (North Karelia)
* Parikkala (South Karelia)
* Rautjärvi (South Karelia)
* Ruokolahti (South Karelia)
* Imatra (South Karelia)
* Lappeenranta (South Karelia)
* Lemi (South Karelia)
* Luumäki (South Karelia)
* Savitaipale (South Karelia)
* Taipalsaari (South Karelia)

I also rode through the center of Liperi, though I had previously ridden along the main road from Joensuu.

This means that we have now visited all 13 municipalities in the region of North Karelia. Some of them we visited on last year's trip eastward ([day 1](../2025-07-07-osterled1/) and [day 2](../2025-07-08-osterled2/)), some [yesterday](../2026-07-13-sydost1/), and the last ones today. As for South Karelia, there are still a few left.
