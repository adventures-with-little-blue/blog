# Orienteering night in Ribäcken

It is Monday evening and time for orienteering practice. As usual, I have Little Blue with me. Today means gravel roads, and on the way home a visit to Solf church.

<!--more-->

This time the orienteering practice takes place in Ribäcken, Malax. It had still been raining heavily in the afternoon, but the long gravel roads in southern Malax were surprisingly dry and in good condition. No great difficulty avoiding the puddles. On the way to the training venue, deep in the forest along Ribäcksvägen, a vintage car stood on display.

![Ribäcken veteran](01-ribacken-veteran.jpg "Vintage car along Ribäcksvägen.")

I am usually there when it starts at 6 pm, but this time I arrived a little later. Even so, there were not many cars there. Partly there do not seem to be any children's courses, and partly the weather may have scared off some of the participants.

![Orienteering](02-orientering.jpg "We are orienteering among wind turbines again. Not many cars are on site.")

It was quite a difficult course: the forest is uniform, everything looks the same, and there were no clear reference points in the terrain. I missed a little at the first three controls, then gave up trying to read the map properly and went almost entirely by compass bearing. I found the remaining controls without any major mistakes, but it was slow going.

After the orienteering I headed back towards Malax along forest roads and small roads. The forest roads are in good condition, surely partly because they were improved when the wind turbines were built. Fun to ride, but not much to see in the forest.

![Lean-to shelter](03-vindskydd.jpg "The hunting club's lean-to shelter.")

![Wind turbine](04-vindmolla.jpg "Yet another wind turbine. They all look the same.")

Once I got out onto a public road, it was more gravel again. And in fact the public roads were overall almost in worse condition than the forest roads, probably because of heavier traffic. But there really are many roads and small villages here south of the Malax river. Långmossen, Bertlasmossa, Börängen and Kopparby. And all the time various forest roads branched off southwards into the woods.

Then I realised that I still had not photographed Solf church, so I made a stop there. [Solf](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solf) (sv) was formerly an independent municipality that also included [Sundom](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundom,_Finland) (sv). In 1973 Sundom was transferred to Vaasa, while Solf together with Korsholm, Kvevlax, Replot and Björköby merged to form the present-day [Korsholm municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korsholm).

![Solf church](05-solf-kyrka1.jpg "Little Blue at Solf church.")

Solf church was inaugurated in 1786 and given the name Church of the Holy Trinity. This church replaced an earlier church building from 1626 that stood right next to it. The site where the old church stood lies just across the road, in a fenced little grove of spruce trees. Otherwise, the premises of Solf parish are gathered in a very compact area. The church, cemetery, vicarage and parish hall all lie in the same area. And right next to it is [Stundars open-air museum](https://stundars.fi), but that will have to wait for another day.
