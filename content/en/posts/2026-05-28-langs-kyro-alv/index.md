# Along the Kyrö River

Thursday evening, and Vaasan Suunnistajat had arranged the orienteering practice in Höstves. The orienteering went really badly this time; I made mistakes at almost every control and was much slower than most of the others at finding them. But the weather was fine, so after the orienteering I continued from Höstves towards Vähäkyrö for a short evening ride.

<!--more-->

Between Veikars in Korsholm and the centre of Ylistaro (now part of Seinäjoki), especially along the river's northern bank, the road follows the course of the Kyro River the whole way, and in many places the river is clearly visible from the road. This is something of a favourite road of mine for a shorter ride. Beautiful, winding, and for the most part with fairly little traffic. Unfortunately the road surface is starting to be quite poor in many places, but not so poor that it takes away the pleasure. This evening it was simply a matter of riding.

On the way I stopped to photograph a few churches. First I completed my collection of photos of Vaasa's churches by photographing [Vähäkyrö Church](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lillkyro_kyrka) (sv).

![Vähäkyrö Church](lillkyro_kyrka.jpg "Vähäkyrö Church")

In Isokyrö, on the northern side of the river, there is the new [Isokyrö Church](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storkyro_kyrka) (sv), built at the end of the 19th century. The old church is on the southern bank; we will return to it on the way home.

![Isokyrö Church](storkyro_kyrka.jpg "Isokyrö Church")

From the centre of Ylistaro you can continue following the river eastwards, but then you either have to ride on gravel roads or make a detour along the dull big road towards Lapua. Today I did not really have time to continue any farther. I photographed [Ylistaro Church](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ylistaron_kirkko) (fi) and then turned back to follow the northern riverbank westwards.

![Ylistaro Church](ylistaro_kyrka.jpg "Ylistaro Church")

I followed the river along Alapää road, then turned away from the river up towards Orisberg. Orisberg and [Orisberg Church](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orisbergin_kirkko) (fi) were the evening's novelty; I had not visited it before. The church building is privately owned and belongs to [Orisberg Manor](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orisberg) (sv). The manor is in private use and not open to the public, but the church and the small surrounding burial ground can be visited. A Christian association runs a cafe next to the church, perhaps worth a visit some time. It looked cozy, but was naturally closed at this time of the evening.

![Orisberg Church](orisbergs_kyrka.jpg "Orisberg Church")

After Orisberg I rode past Isokyrö railway station and through Orismala back to the main road by the river. I rode through the centre of Isokyrö to the old church, [St. Lars Church](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/S:t_Lars_kyrka,_Storkyro) (sv). The oldest part of this stone church was built in 1514. However, there had already been a wooden church on the same site, which was gradually replaced by the stone church. The original church was built in 1304, or possibly even at the end of the 13th century. Nowadays a yearly 18th-century market is held in the area around the church.

![St. Lars Church, Isokyrö](storkyro_gamla_kyrka.jpg "St. Lars Church, Isokyrö Old Church.")

After that I headed straight home along the main road. Dull, but slightly faster than the river route. This evening's ride came to 137 km.
