# Season Opening 2026 - Sommarösund

The spring sun is shining and the snow melted a long time ago. Every night still brings frost, but by afternoon the roads have been dry and temperatures have been above +5°C for nearly a month now. The reason this year's season opener was delayed is various issues with the bike. But after wrenching all day, it felt like the bike was in good enough shape for a first ride.

Since all the snow melted long ago, the front wheel headed out over the Replot bridge this time, hoping to find some sea ice to photograph the bike against. On the way I noticed I was hungry, so the first stop was [Berny's](https://www.berny.fi/en/). Important to watch the closing time so you don't miss out on pizza!

<!--more-->

![Replotbron](bernys.jpg "The Replot bridge seen from Berny's parking lot.")

After the pizza I continued out through Replot, turned off towards Vallgrund and then further out to Sommarösund. There wasn't much ice, but a thin crust remained in the bay. It was probably the last chance to photograph the bike with sea ice for this winter.

![Sommarösund](sommarosund1.jpg "Sommarösund still had a little ice left.")

![Coastguard station](sommarosund2.jpg "Vallgrund coastguard station")

On the way home I took a small detour via Jungsund, Karperö and then through Kvevlax to Golkas. I got home just as the sun went down. The season opener was 129 km long.

![Kolkki bridge](golkas.jpg "At the Kolkki bridge")

## How did it feel?

Finally out riding. I didn't feel particularly rusty — it felt like I had good control of the motorcycle.

The bike, on the other hand, still has quite a bit to sort out. It seems there'll be more wrenching ahead.

* The front tire has a slow leak. If you start with full 2 bar, the pressure has dropped to maybe 1.7 bar by the time you get home. As the pressure drops, the vibrations in the handlebars increase. I'll probably need to order a new front tire regardless — it's getting quite worn after more than 21,000 km.

* The rear tire is also almost done, after only about 11,000 km. It's very square, just like the previous one got at a too-early stage. Maybe I should run higher tire pressure (the manufacturer specifies 2.25 bar without passenger, 2.5 bar with). Or I need to learn to take corners properly, to wear away the "chicken stripes", so to speak.

* One of the rear brake pads is almost completely worn. Needs replacing. And the fact that only one pad is worn while the other has plenty of material left suggests the brake caliper — and particularly the pins — should be cleaned or rebuilt.

* Idle too low, the bike almost stalls. Should be easy to fix, just adjust it manually.

* The choke is sticking. If you open it fully, it seems to stay on full. Need to try lubricating the cable.

* In general the engine feels a bit "unwilling". Maybe the carbs need adjusting and syncing. I did try vacuum syncing already, but it didn't go well because the fuel flow isn't even enough with the tank off (the fuel tap needs vacuum to work). I bought a cheap garage tank, but it leaks like crazy — cheap junk.

* The front forks are leaking slightly. Already tried cleaning them with a thin plastic film. The right got better, the left is still leaking. Will probably need a rebuild at some point.

* The heated grip on the throttle side isn't heating, or at least significantly less than the left side. Really annoying — those Oxford heated grips have done nothing but cause trouble the whole time.

Sigh, will it be more wrenching than riding this season?
