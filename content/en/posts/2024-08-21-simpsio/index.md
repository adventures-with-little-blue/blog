# Malkakoski and Simpsiö

From the archives: a few photos from a ride to Malkakoski and Simpsiö.

<!--more-->

### Malkakoski

Malkakoski in Ylistaro (Seinäjoki) is said to be Finland's largest artificial rapids. A mecca for canoeists. It was created to prevent flooding. The dam can be raised and lowered to regulate the water flow.

![Malkakoski](malkakoski2.jpg "Malkakoski.")

![Malkakoski](malkakoski3.jpg "Malkakoski's adjustable dam.")

![Memorial](malkakoski1.jpg "A memorial to the toil of past generations. It is on the northern bank of the rapids.")


### Simpsiö

Simpsiö in Lapua offers the longest ski slopes in South Ostrobothnia.

![Simpsiö](simpsio1.jpg "The ski slope at Simpsiö.")

The viewing tower at the top of Simpsiö Hill offers a fine view of the surroundings. The viewing platform is about 100 m above the surrounding plains. According to my notes from the ride, the tower was noticeably swaying despite the rather light wind.

![Simpsiö](simpsio2.jpg "The viewing tower at Simpsiö.")

![Simpsiö](simpsio5.jpg "View towards the ski slope.")

![Simpsiö](simpsio3.jpg "View.")

![Simpsiö](simpsio4.jpg "View.")


My notes say that it was a pleasant 205 km evening ride via Jurva - Ilmajoki - Malkakoski - Simpsiö - Ylistaro - Laihia.
