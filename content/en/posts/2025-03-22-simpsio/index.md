# With the bike to the ski slope

Time for the year's first ride with Little Blue. Not actually the very first one, I had managed a small test ride earlier. Regardless, it feels absolutely insanely early to start riding a motorbike in mid-March, but what do you do when the sun is shining and the main roads are already dry? Along for the ride on the pillion seat sits my daughter. She also suffers from a bit of motorcycle fever, but as a fifteen-year-old, she's not allowed to ride herself yet.

New for the day was a small extra spoiler on the windscreen – because despite the sunshine, it gets cold quickly from the wind at speed as long as the temperature is below +10. The spoiler's most important task is to reduce some of the turbulence around the helmet and thus reduce the wind noise. I thought it made the situation a bit better, though not a huge improvement.

The journey went on the north side of the Kyrö river from Kolkki to Ylistaro – my favourite stretch for a shorter ride. From Ylistaro we took the main road towards Lapua and then turned off towards Simpsiö. We stopped and had coffee at [Simpsiö ski centre](https://www.simpsio.com/en/). The new restaurant below the ski slope is fresh! And we took the opportunity to take some photos. A bit odd with the motorcycle in front of all the eager downhill skiers on the slope. I exchanged a few words with a retired biker who thought we were brave/crazy to be out on two wheels at this time of year.

![Bike in front of the ski slope](simpsio.jpg)

After the coffee break we continued along the tarmac road in the direction of the Kyrö river and Malkamäki. Despite it being afternoon and over +5 degrees, there seemed to be a hint of ice still on the road surface, so we had to take it easy.

We stopped at [Malkakoski])(https://retkeilelakeuksilla.fi/retkikohteet/seinajoki/malkakoski/), by the Kyrö river, and admired the roaring journey of the meltwater towards the sea. The river isn't ice-free yet, but in the rapids it's full speed. Nature's power is impressive – even though the rapids themselves are artificial.

![Little Blue at Malkakoski](malkakoski1.jpg)

![Enjoying the early spring](malkakoski2.jpg)

Then it was time to head home. First to Ylistaro along tarmac roads and then along the south side of the Kyrö river this time. We avoided the main road and tried to follow the river as closely as possible. Must say though that Alapääntie/Vainionraitti from Ylistaro to Valtaala was quite rough. At regular intervals there are proper bumps in the tarmac. Not pleasant when there are two of us on Little Blue. We also rode through Isokyrö centre and past the old church. Then a stretch on the main road before we turned off towards Vähäkyrö. Finally via Toby and home.

In total it was 180 km. Despite the temperature being around +5, I didn't get particularly cold. My daughter had quite thin gloves and got a bit cold in her hands, but otherwise it went fine.
