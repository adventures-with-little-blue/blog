# Sea View

The previous battery, only a year old, was deep discharged after someone had left the power on with the driving lights. Now it no longer accepts a charge. So I had to buy a new battery. And to test it and make sure everything was working, I took a little trip out to the archipelago.

<!--more-->

I rode out to Klobbskat. There I climbed up the observation tower. Beautiful, but quite cold. Then coffee and a bun at [Cafe & Restaurant Compass](https://www.facebook.com/CafeCompass/).

![Klobbskat](klobbskat1.jpg)

![Klobbskat](klobbskat2.jpg)

After that, a short extra ride through Södra Vallgrund out to Sommarö Sund. Then back home via Jungsund, Karperö, Smedsby, Ristinummi and Runsor.

![Sommarö Sund](sommarosund.jpg)

It was a short trip of 172 km. The new battery works, but it still loses charge even on a relatively short ride. Something is wrong with Lilla Blue's electrical system.

I also noticed that it's time to change the rear tire. After only 10,000 km, my Roadtec 01 rear tire is completely, totally worn out! The tire was almost new when I bought the bike, hardly ridden a few hundred kilometers. Feels like a very short lifespan for a tire, especially since the bike isn't particularly heavy and I haven't exactly abused them by burning rubber or anything like that.
