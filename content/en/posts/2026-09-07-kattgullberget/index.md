# Monday orienteering and a rainy ride

Orienteering training again. Just as I started riding home after orienteering, it began to rain. Despite the rain, I couldn't resist taking a little extra ride.

<!--more-->

Not much to tell, really. Orienteering at Långåminne went partly terribly and partly well. The bad part was a strained calf. I strained my calf the last time I went orienteering, three weeks ago. I thought it would have recovered. I set off, and it felt really good to be moving again. Then, after barely 100 metres, my calf muscle popped again and the pain was back. That was unfortunately probably my last orienteering session for the year. Now at least a month's rest awaits, and then the season is over.

And the good part? Well, I still walked through the entire course. I went more or less straight to every control (one small error of a minute or so). The course was very easy, admittedly, but perhaps you have time to read the map a little more carefully when you're not moving at speed.

![Rain](regnigt_visir.jpg "Rain on the visor.")

It didn't rain while I was in the forest. Just as I got on the bike and set off, the rain began. Not heavy rain, but a steady, persistent drizzle. I could have headed home by the shortest route. But that wasn't what happened. Maybe it's some kind of withdrawal symptom, or perhaps there is a little wiring fault somewhere, but I ended up taking a short extra ride despite the rain. It was actually really nice - fairly warm (around +15°C), and my riding gear easily handled a spell of rainy weather. The ride went via Kråkbacken, Havras and Kyrkbacken in Malax, and then out to Åminne.
