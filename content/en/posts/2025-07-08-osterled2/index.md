## Finland's rainiest point?

_Day two of my three-day journey eastward._

### Introduction

During the trip's first day I rode from my home to Lakkala campsite in Koli National Park. The plan for day two was to visit Finland's easternmost point, Virmajärvi in Ilomantsi. This is also the European Union's mainland easternmost point, at least until Ukraine joins the union.

<!--more-->

![Today's route](koli-konnevesi.webp)

### Toward Virmajärvi

The weather report promised rain from 8 o'clock, so I had set the alarm for 7 to get to pack away a relatively dry tent. Well, at 6 o'clock it started pouring, so everything was probably as wet as it could be when it was to be packed. And after rushing around in the rain with the packing, I was also already quite wet when I set off toward Virmajärvi.

The journey went through [Patvinsuo National Park](https://www.luontoon.fi/en/destinations/patvinsuo-national-park) and then onward along gravel roads toward Hattuvaara village in Ilomantsi. In places the road was really slippery and at some point it was actually close to the front wheel slipping away from me. Along the way I rode past Endomine's gold mine in Hosko and also near their enrichment facility in Pampalo. Both places were of course closed to visitors and seen like that from a distance didn't look particularly exciting at all.

In Hattuvaara I looked at the [eukterion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eukterion) (tsasouna), the local orthodox prayer house, and then I took a coffee break at [Taistelijan Talo](https://taistelijantalo.fi/en) ("The Fighters' House"). There was a museum about the [Battle of Ilomantsi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Ilomantsi_(1944)) in the final stage of the Continuation War where Finnish forces annihilated a many times larger Russian attacking force and thereby prevented a devastating breakthrough. I skipped the museum anyway, because I was at this stage already more or less soaked through and it didn't appeal to trudge around in wet motorcycle clothes.

Then I headed almost straight east toward [Lake Virmajärvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Virmayarvi). I saw some RVs on the road, but there was certainly no congestion. I was alone at the actual easternmost point. Sure it was probably beautiful, but in this weather it was more of a "been-there-done-that-check" than anything else. The gravel road wasn't in particularly good condition either, with quite large stones and in places soft and in places slippery in the rain. Now it would really have been good to be on a real adventure bike with better suspension and coarser tires.

### Trouble with the bike, trouble with the phone

Somewhere on the way back to Hattuvaara I heard loud noise from the rear of the bike. I stopped and checked, but saw nothing just then. Probably I had already lost the swingarm's right end piece by then, whose purpose is to keep the rear wheel stable. I noticed it was missing only later, but I had noticed that it felt if possible even more unstable to ride back toward Hattuvaara than on the way there. Without the end piece the rear tire wobbles slightly even on asphalt, it feels a bit like riding on gravel. When you ride on gravel it's of course even worse. Although it felt a bit strange to ride, there was still no acute risk that the wheel would fall off or anything, so I had to ride all the way home without this part.

![Swingarm](svingarm.jpg)
The missing component (picture taken another day)

I continued in the pouring rain to Ilomantsi center. Phone and the display with Android Auto guided me into S-Market's ABC to refuel but when I took out the phone to pay for fuel, the phone was completely dead. At that stage I assumed it was the moisture that caused the phone to die. No problem with the refueling, I got to pay with card instead of with the ABC app, but now I was left without navigation, I had no paper map. Nor did I have any way to let the family know I was fine, despite not answering the phone.

After a short tour of the city I had a coffee and a think at [Hermannin viinitorni](https://hermannin.fi/en/hermanni/viinitorni/:language/en) ("Hermanni's Wine Tower"), a bar up in an old water tower. Here it must be said that the view from the bar's outdoor terrace would have been phenomenal if you could just see anything through the driving rain. All of Ilomantsi with surroundings is probably beautiful. Should be revisited in better weather. Unfortunately it was driving rain the whole time and moreover I had mostly thoughts on the newly arisen problem.

New phone, it had to be. The helpful young ladies in the bar showed on Google Maps where Gigantti is in Joensuu, it would hardly be any problem to find my way there even without a map. So I headed there next. A new phone was purchased and also a screen protector and a phone case from Prisma, which lies convininently right next to Gigantti. Then I sat there in soaking wet motorcycle gear at Prisma in Joensuu and installed the new phone. Unfortunately I didn't know the crucial passwords by heart and without passwords no apps. But I could at least let the family know I was safe.

Now much time had been used thinking about phones. I skipped driving through Joensuu center and instead steered toward [New Valamo monastery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Valamo) in Heinävesi. There I ate a very late and actually quite tasteless lunch and then continued the journey.

### Steering toward the sun

The plan was to drive through Varkaus and then via Sorsakoski to Leppävirta, but since brighter weather was promised only toward the Leppävirta direction, I skipped Varkaus. And sure enough, on the way toward Leppävirta the pouring rain first turned to drizzle and then stopped completely. I crossed the magnificent Leppävirta bridge without bothering to stop and photograph and instead I turned toward Suonenjoki. A bit north of Leppävirta I finally got to take off the rain gear and boot covers and change the totally soaked winter gloves for almost dry summer gloves – by this time the sun was already shining and I started drying up quickly in the headwind.

Suonenjoki didn't seem to be a particularly exciting place, but still there was some adventure awaiting me. I refueled paying with card, which means I got off the bike (when I refuel with mobile I usually don't get off). When I was about to step over and up on the bike and in the same motion take it off the stand, I managed to tip it over. It was a maneuver I had done quite a few times without any problems, but now, tired after the day's hardships, I managed to get the motorcycle to tip over. On a couple of earlier tip-over occasions, I had had managed to lift up the bike without any problems, but fully fueled and with packing it was actually really heavy and I really struggled. No damage, except that the side mirror came loose when I lifted – I just had to tighten it again.

Then it was time for the day's last disappointment. I rode through Rautalampi and into [Southern Konnevesi National Park](https://www.luontoon.fi/en/destinations/southern-konnevesi-national-park). I had picked out a place to spend the night, but it turned out it wasn't possible to get there with the motorcycle (at least not without driving over private landowners' roads and driving past a potentially closed barrier). I didn't want to be "that guy" (again), so I gave up on the national park and instead rode to [Häyrylänranta Camping](https://hayrylanranta.fi/in-english/) in Konnevesi. And there I had probably more than just a little luck. I hadn't noticed that it was already past 21, so the campsite had actually already closed. But the last employee was just about to close for the day and she kindly promised to let me pitch my tent and also gave me access to the shower facilities.

![Häyrylänranta Camping, Konnevesi](konnevesi.jpg)

A warm shower didn't perhaps completely compensate for missing the planned tour on the Vuori-Kalaja hiking trail, but it sure felt great to get dry underwear on. The outer garments had largely dried from the headwind, except for the boots. So the evening ended with me eating grilled sausage and drinking red wine while I discussed Life, the Universe and Everything with Konnevesi resident Eero, who had found his way down to the campsite's barbecue area.

* Ilomantsi (Northern Karelia)
* Liperi (Northern Karelia)
* Heinävesi (Northern Karelia)
* Leppävirta (Northern Savonia)
* Suonenjoki (Northern Savonia)
* Rautalampi (Northern Savonia)
