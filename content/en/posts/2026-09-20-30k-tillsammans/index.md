# A small look back at 30,000 km of motorcycling

Somewhere along Velkuantie, on the way from Lempisaari (Swedish: Villnäs, Finnish: Askainen) towards Merimasku, near the municipal boundary between Masku and Naantali, Little Blue and I reached a milestone. We have now ridden more than 30,000 km together. Approximately, at least. The odometer read 98,749 km at the end of the day. This is now our fourth riding season together, although the first season was only a little over two weeks long.

<!--more-->

### Season 2023

I bought Little Blue on 30 September 2023, the day after I passed my riding test and received my temporary category A licence. After that, we were out as often as I had the opportunity. There was some riding in cold weather, once even in sleet. We had time to experience fingers stiff from the cold, pouring rain and riding in the dark. The last ride of the year was on 14 October, after which it started snowing. During the first season, we rode 1,030 km together.

### Season 2024

The 2024 season started late, not until May, because all the tinkering I had planned to do during the winter was of course postponed until the sun began to shine and the bare asphalt began to beckon. Among other things, I adjusted the valve clearances and cleaned the carburettors. We made our very first long trip at the beginning of June, a [two-day trip to Espoo](../2024-06-09-helsingfors/). After that, the rest of the season was mostly shorter trips and everyday riding. We did make one slightly longer trip, up to [Småbönders](../2024-08-17-smabonders/) and back. The season ended when it started getting far too muddy, as late as the first of November, after 8,343 kilometres ridden.

### Season 2025

The following season began record early. The asphalt dried up even though snow remained in the drifts. We took our first trip in mid-March. On the second ride, my daughter came along on a trip to [Simpsiö](../2025-03-22-simpsio/), where we stood watching skiers swooshing down the slope. Then I got to see for myself that spring does not arrive as early in the [Lappajärvi area](../2025-03-23-tour-lappajarvi/) as it does on the coast – icy, rutted roads were scary. In any case, a 350 km trip in March is not bad. During the spring, I got to analyse and fix [electrical problems](../2025-05-23-charger/). There were several day trips of between 400 and 700 km, including to [Rauma](../2025-05-30-raumo/). The summer's great adventure was the three-day trip to [Koli](../2025-07-07-osterled1/), [Virmajärvi](../2025-07-08-osterled2/) and [Tikkakoski](../2025-07-09-osterled3/) at the beginning of July, with tent accommodation and everything. At the end of July, we then made our attempt at the [Iron Butt](../2025-07-31-ironbutt/), when we rode 1,836 km in a day. We managed one last long day trip to [Tampere](../2025-09-06-tour-tammerfors/) at the beginning of September. Then the season ended abruptly and disappointingly with engine failure on 11 September. Coolant ran out onto the garage floor, and it turned out that a [new head gasket and water pump](../2026-03-29-topplockspackning/) were needed. In 2025, we rode a full 11,103 km, a record that will probably stand at least until there is time to take a trip abroad.

### Season 2026

As in previous years, it was difficult to get the tinkering done during the winter, so the 2026 riding season also began with springtime wrenching. The head gasket and water pump had been replaced by the end of March, but the [first trip](../2026-04-12-sommarosund/) was not until 12 April – by then there was not much ice and snow left to find. Overall, there was almost more tinkering than riding at the start of the season, including with tyres, indicators, heated grips and front forks. Most rides involved going to [orienteering training](../2026-05-04-mandagsbommen/) and then taking a short ride afterwards. In mid-July, we did at least set off on this summer's great adventure: 2,903 km over four days with tent accommodation in south-eastern Finland, via among other places [Savonlinna](../2026-07-13-sydost1/), [Lappeenranta](../2026-07-14-sydost2/), [Hamina](../2026-07-15-sydost3/) and [Puumala](../2026-07-16-sydost4/). At the beginning of August, we made a long day trip through [Kanta-Häme and Pirkanmaa](../2026-08-08-natur-glas-bilar/). At the end of August, we made a three-day trip down to Helsinki, this time through [Päijät-Häme](../2026-08-20-hfors1/), [Uusimaa](../2026-08-21-hfors2/) and [Kanta-Häme](../2026-08-22-hfors3/). The year's last long day trip was probably the trip to [Turku](../2026-09-19-abo/), but the whole season is not over yet. There are some plans, but they are quite weather-dependent this late in the autumn. So far this year, we have ridden 9,936 km, so we should certainly reach 10,000 km.

### Project Finland/308 and what comes next

In spring 2025, I started the [Finland/308](/finland308/) project: visiting all of Finland's municipalities together with Little Blue. We did not start completely from zero, but did count the municipalities we had already visited in 2024. During 2025, we counted every municipality in which we had ridden even one metre, but from 2026 onward I changed the rules so that I must have a photo of something recognisable from every municipality we have visited. In practice, the easiest thing to photograph is a church, since every municipality has at least one church. I am putting together a page with photos of churches, but it is not quite ready to publish yet.

With 98,749 km on the odometer, Little Blue, with her small 500 cc engine, is beginning to count as an old lady. This winter or spring, the brakes need to be gone through, the steering bearing replaced, the carburettors cleaned and more. Let us hope she has the strength for one more season. Right now, the engine is not running quite right, but perhaps we can get everything sorted out. With a dash of realism, it may well be that we will not have time to complete our Finland/308 project before retirement awaits her. Completing the project will still require several really long trips to reach the last municipalities we have left to visit: from Åland to Hanko and from Hailuoto to Kuhmo, Kuopio, Rovaniemi and above all the very northernmost parts of Lapland. We will hardly have time for all that next summer, nor will we fit it into another 10,000 km.
