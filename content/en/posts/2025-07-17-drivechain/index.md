# New Chain and Sprockets

Time to change the chain and sprockets. There are rattling noises coming from the sprocket, especially if the chain isn't perfectly tensioned. The chain also moves quite a lot sideways on the sprocket. I guess this is the price for not maintaining things very carefully. I've ridden just over 16,000 km with this chain, but it probably wasn't brand new when I bought the bike. Shouldn't a chain last much longer than that?
