# First Time Through Småbönders

From the archives: a couple of photos from one of my first longer rides.

<!--more-->

It turned out to be a fairly long day ride via Laihia - Kauhava - Kortesjärvi - Evijärvi - Inansaari - Småbönders - Veteli - Kaustinen - Terjärv - Kållby - Bennäs - Sundby - Nykarleby - Munsala - Hirvlax - Kantlax - Kimo - Röukas - Kalapää - Vörå - Vähäkyrö. A total of 431 km. I also stopped for a few hours at a birthday party along the way. Unfortunately, I only took a couple of photos.


### Småbönders

![Welcome to Småbönders](smabonders1.jpg "Welcome to Småbönders.")

This was the first time I rode through this village. The predominantly Swedish-speaking village of [Småbönders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sm%C3%A5b%C3%B6nders) in Kronoby reaches quite far eastwards between the entirely Finnish-speaking municipalities of Evijärvi, Veteli and Kaustinen. It somehow feels like the most remote point in Swedish-speaking Ostrobothnia – perhaps together with Sideby in the south. Småbönders is at least the Swedish-speaking village farthest from the coast.

![Småbönders](smabonders2.jpg "At Lake Svartsjön in Småbönders.")


### Vörå

There are not really any public roads from Lake Röukas to Lake Kalapää in Vörå. I ended up on some forest roads with prohibition signs. But when you have planned the route in advance and suddenly come to a prohibition sign that would mean a 30–40 minute detour, it feels a little unnecessary. This time I rode straight through, but next time I will know that it is prohibited. I apologise to anyone who was offended – I met absolutely no one along the way.


### New Municipalities

On this trip, Little Blue and I visited the municipalities of Lapua, Evijärvi and Veteli together for the first time. This was actually before the [Finland/308](/finland308/) project had started (Little Blue and I are trying to visit every municipality in Finland), but since we have been there, we count the visits.
