# The Wolf Cave in Kristinestad

Spring truly arrived at Vappu (May Day). Both May Day Eve and May Day itself were warm and sunny. Today too, Saturday the second of May, started out sunny and warm. A perfect day for a ride, and today I headed for the ["Wolf Cave"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susiluola_Cave) (Varggrottan) in Kristinestad.

<!--more-->

This wasn't planned at all, really, but since the weather was brilliant and there was nothing too pressing to do, I decided to go for a ride. Most longer rides I usually plan quite carefully in advance, but this time I just punched a few waypoints into the computer to avoid having to wander around during the actual ride. With temperatures between 10–15°C I put on thermal base layers, and the winter liner in the jacket and trousers is still in place (it'll probably stay in until July). But I at least took summer gloves (with thicker gloves in the tank bag). I lubed the chain, checked the tyre pressures and quickly threw together a water bottle, first aid kit and whatever else you need for a few hours' ride. Then off we went.

The first short stop was at [Korsnäs church](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korsn%C3%A4s_kyrka) (sv). For my [Finland/308](/finland308/) project I've started thinking that I really ought to have photographic evidence from every municipality. And what is there in absolutely every municipality? A church, or several. So why not start photographing every church I ride past? Maybe it'll turn into a collection of photos of all Finland's churches, or at least a good number of them? So I might as well start photographing nearby churches too, to make the collection complete, so to speak.

![Korsnäs church](korsnas_kyrka.jpg "The Korsnäs church")

The summer gloves felt a bit chilly, even though the new Koso heated grips heat evenly and well. I took the opportunity to switch to the leather gloves.

I swung by the Harrström beach pavilion — nowadays a rather sad sight. Then on to Norrnäs and from there along a paved (but terribly uneven) road to Yttermark — a road I hadn't ridden before.

![Harrström pavilion](harrstrom_paviljong.jpg "Harrström pavilion. Run-down, no longer in use?")

Then a quick visit to [Närpes church](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%A4rpes_kyrka) (sv) and the church stables. Then on via Skrattnäsvägen towards Kristinestad. It wasn't on the map, but I came up with the idea of a short detour via [Carlsro](https://www.kristinestad.fi/leisure-and-wellbeing/museivasendet-en-us/stadsmuseum-carlsro-en-us/), the Kristinestad city museum. It was closed, but beautiful on the outside as well.

![Närpes church](narpes_kyrka.jpg "The Närpes church")

![Carlsro](carlsro.jpg "Carlsro, the Kristinestad city museum.")

In Kristinestad I photographed the old church, [Ulrika Eleonora church](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulrika_Eleonora_kyrka,_Kristinestad) (sv), and the [new church](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristinestads_kyrka,_Finland) (sv). Then out of the city and on to the large [Lappfjärd church](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lappfj%C3%A4rds_kyrka) (sv), reportedly the second largest rural church in Finland according to Wikipedia (probably second to [Kerimäki church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerim%C3%A4ki_Church)). From Lappfjärd I continued via Perus and Dagsmark towards Karijoki.

![Ulrika Eleonora church](kstad_kyrka1.jpg "The Ulrika Eleonora church")

![Kristinestad new church](kstad_kyrka2.jpg "Kristinestad's new church")

![Lappfjärd church](lappfjard_kyrka.jpg "The Lappfjärd church")

Then it was time for the highlight of the trip (literally). The ["Wolf Cave"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susiluola_Cave) is a short distance from the centre of Karijoki, but on the Kristinestad side of the municipal border. To get the shortest possible walk, I first rode 2.5 km of gravel road — the fun kind. I parked as close as possible and left my jacket with the bike. Now I was starting to regret putting on long johns. After a mere 150 m walk I was already quite warm. The Wolf Cave itself wasn't that spectacular — a rock crevice with a fence and some signs of a very slow ongoing excavation. But: been there, done that.

![Wolf Cave fence](varggrottan1.jpg "The Wolf Cave fence")

![Wolf Cave, excavation in progress](varggrottan2.jpg "Wolf Cave, excavation in progress")

Then I pushed on a few hundred more metres to a small lookout tower on top of Vargberget (the "Wolf Hill"). Nice, but not exactly spectacular. But as mentioned, this was the highest point of the trip. The landscape on Wolf Hill is actually quite beautiful in itself, with scattered boulders on otherwise fairly sandy terrain. Everything was very dry. And as I said, quite warm in my winter-lined Gore-Tex riding trousers. I switched back to the summer gloves — and with the Koso on its lowest setting, my fingers didn't get cold on the way home.

In Karijoki I photographed [Karijoki church](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karijoen_kirkko) (fi) and then continued towards Teuva via Luovankylä. Now came a few kilometres of the less fun kind of gravel — freshly laid with loose chippings. I'm awful at riding on roads like this; I tense up, don't know how much grip I have and don't really dare test it either. But I assume the more I ride on this kind of surface, the better I'll get. The road took me past [Parra ski centre](https://parra.fi/en/) — a little snow remained, though nothing compared to the contrast between skiers and motorcycle on [last year's first bike ride to Simpsiö](/posts/2025-03-22-simpsio/). Then the remaining stretch of asphalt road into Östermark was really fun to ride!

![Karijoki church](botom_kyrka.jpg "The Karijoki church")

![Parra](parra.jpg "Parra ski centre with a little snow left on the slopes.")

In Teuva I photographed both the [new church](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teuvan_kirkko) (fi) and the Teuva church ruin ([Teuvan rauniokirkko](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teuvan_rauniokirkko)). The old church was destroyed by fire in 1950, but part of it has been restored and is used as an outdoor stage. From Teuva the journey continued via Norinkylä to the [Jurva church](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurvan_kirkko) (fi). And then on to the [Laihia church](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laihian_kirkko) (fi) in Laihia. The road from Pyörni in Jurva to Kylänpää in Laihela is fun — it's winding and lightly trafficked and for now the surface is in fairly good condition.

![The Teuva church](ostermark_kyrka.jpg "The Teuva church")

![The Teuva church ruin](ostermark_kyrkoruin.jpg "The Teuva church ruin (Teuvan rauniokirkko)")

![The Laihia church](laihela_kyrka.jpg "The Laihia church")

It turned out to be a 279 km ride with photos of 9 churches and one church ruin. The Koso heated grips work great. The bike otherwise runs fairly well — it feels like the engine is running slightly unevenly and slightly hotter than before, so I should probably adjust and synchronise the carburettors. And then the front tyre is leaking a little, which caused some vibrations towards the end of the trip.
