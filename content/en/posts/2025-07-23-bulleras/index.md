## Testing a beach: Bullerås beach in Södra Vallgrund

It's still hot, so what's better than a trip to the beach? Today I rode off to [Bullerås beach](https://korsholm.fi/valfard/fritidsverksamhet/simstrander-och-friluftsbad/sodra-vallgrund-simstrander) in Södra Vallgrund.

<!--more-->

I had actually never visited this beach before. Actually, I was on my way there with the bike once before, but I turned around just before I arrived. To get to the beach you have to drive over a private yard. Last time I didn't really know where the beach was located and didn't want to disturb people unnecessarily.

### How to get there

The road to Bullerås goes via the boring Alskat road, over the magnificent Replot bridge, into Replot church village where you turn toward Vallgrund. Then you follow the quite winding asphalt road until it's time to turn off on a gravel road toward Kalvholm, and after a short stretch you turn again toward Bullerås. After a few kilometers of gravel road you come, as mentioned, to a sign that this is a private yard, but you just continue on, of course carefully and with respect for the residents.

### Beach rating

The beach itself then? A small sandy patch that's being overgrown by all the reeds. Not so impressive. But there are changing rooms in fine condition (+), toilet (+), barbecue area (+), picnic table (+) and trash bin (+). And a swing set, if you need one (+). Right next to it there's also a guest harbor with a proper dock.

![Bullerås beach](bulleras1.jpg)
At the beach.

I myself skipped the shallow beach and jumped in from the guest harbor's dock. That's hardly what you're supposed to do, as you end up right in the small boat channel. On the other hand, there wasn't such a rush that I disturbed anyone. The water was relatively clear and really warm and nice (+). The ladder up to the high dock barely reached down to the water, so it was a bit tricky to get up again.

![Bullerås beach view](bulleras2.jpg)
View toward the horizon.

*Summary:* Decent facilities but the beach isn't much to write home about.

*Recommendation:* If you wander there, enjoy the view for a while. If it's warm but not too crowded at the dock, then take a dip by all means. Hardly worth a trip just for the beach's sake though.

*Rating:* 2.0/5

### Conclusion

On the way home I rode down through Södra Vallgrund to Sommarösund. Maybe one should test the beach there too? Another time. Then via Jungsund and Karperö to Botniahallen, where my daughter had just finished football practice. Then home. Today's total was 110 km and a cooling dip.
