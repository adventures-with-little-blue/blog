# A short evening ride after work

The weather was fine, so I took the opportunity to go for a short evening ride after work. We rode out to [Meteoria](https://meteoria.fi/en/), in the middle of [Söderfjärden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%B6derfj%C3%A4rden), and then to the pumping station in Munsmo.

<!--more-->

Söderfjärden is a meteorite crater that was formed when an asteroid struck here around 520 million years ago. The crater is now roughly five to six kilometres across and has an almost circular shape. Just over a hundred years ago, it was still a shallow sea bay, but now fields stretch across the old crater floor.

![View over Söderfjärden's fields](meteorian3.jpg "View over Söderfjärden's fields.")

The drainage of Söderfjärden took place between 1919 and 1927 and was the largest drainage project in Northern Europe at the time. The embankment, basin, ditches and canals were dug with shovels and manual labour, and the first pumping station was built. The pumps could be started during the spring flood in 1926, and the work was completed the following year. The open lake had then been transformed into 1,430 hectares of new farmland.

![Memorial stone for the drainage of Söderfjärden](meteorian2.jpg "Memorial stone for the drainage of Söderfjärden")


### Meteoria

Meteoria is a visitor centre that opened in 2008 in the middle of Söderfjärden. It has exhibitions about the history of the place and the Earth, an astronomical observatory and a birdwatching tower with outdoor exhibitions. The centre is run by the Meteoria section of the Sundom Village Association, while the observatory is managed by Vasa Andromeda.

### Söderfjärden pumping station

The new red-brick pump house was built in 1964. Its large electric pumps share the space with artworks. Eivor Holm from Solf, then 16 years old, painted three murals depicting the wetland landscape of the 19th century, the drainage work of the 1920s and the grain store of the 1960s. Later, more works by local artists were added to the building, including Paula Blåfield, Tapani Tammenpää, Nils Nygren, Lars Nedergård and Kaj Smeds.
