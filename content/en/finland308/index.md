### What counts as a visit?

Everything counts here! If we've ridden one meter across the municipal border, we're more than happy to check it off!

Jokes aside, I try to visit the municipality centre or at least some significant district or village. Ideally I want some memento from the place in question. Since 2026, I also make sure to photograph at least one church in every municipality I visit. But for the time being I still count it as a visit even if I have only crossed the municipal border along a short stretch of country road.

My confessions of sin:
* The main road passes along the edge of Taivalkoski - I never stopped.
* The main road passes along the edge of Posio - I never stopped.
* I blasted through all of Oulu on the motorway - I did not stop until Kempele.
* and surely several more

But I will try to visit these municipalities again in due course.


### Municipal mergers

There is a clear trend in Finland for municipalities to merge and become ever larger and fewer. At the time of writing I do not know of any upcoming mergers, but surely more are on the way. Actually managing to visit all 308 municipalities is therefore also a race against the calendar: will we make it before a municipality ceases to exist?

As a small footnote, the map above shows Pertunmaa as a municipality of its own, even though it was merged into Mäntyharju at the beginning of 2025. I have not yet had time to update my (stolen) map material. I did not make it there before Pertunmaa disappeared as a municipality of its own.
